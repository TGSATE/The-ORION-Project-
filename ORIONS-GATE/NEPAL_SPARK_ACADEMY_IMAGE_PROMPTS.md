## The idea behind the pictures (one minute)

Your energy map already says: **Kronos** 🔥 = fire, kinetic chaos, connection; **Orion** ⚡ = lightning, focus, clarity; **KRION** 🌍 = the balanced, Earth-green energy where the two meet (the Garden, the Playground, the Gate). So the pictures tell one story:

| | 🐉 **Glass Dragon** | 🐖 **Steel Pig** |
|---|---|---|
| **Energy (proposed)** | **Orion ⚡**: light, focus, clarity | **Kronos 🔥**: fire, forge, warmth, people |
| **Why it fits** | Glass bends light into rainbows. A dragon sees far. It's the team of **ideas, art, writing, AI tools, apps and games**. | Steel is forged in fire. A pig is sturdy, clever and loves company. It's the team of **cooking, health, business, family, social media and making things that last**. |
| **Colours** | clear crystal, ice blue, violet, white lightning | brushed steel, ember orange, copper, warm red |
| **Builds** | a glass-and-light **Idea Workshop** | a steel-and-fire **Community Forge** |

**Together they make KRION 🌍**: lightning and fire meet and turn **green-gold**, like new leaves and sunrise. That energy flows *outwards* into the village: clean water, a shared garden, a phone-skills class for grandparents, songs, stories, small businesses. **That's the "healing for their immediate communities"**: people helping people, not medicine.

## Style notes to paste at the end of any prompt

> Style: warm, hopeful, hand-painted fantasy illustration with soft cinematic light. Nepal's real landscape: Himalayan peaks, terraced green hills, rhododendron blossoms (Nepal's national flower), Kathmandu Valley brick courtyards with carved wooden windows. Young adult students shown from behind or at a distance, faces not detailed. No real people, no logos, no brand names. Any text in English only, spelled exactly: "NEPAL SPARK ACADEMY".

**Why those last lines:**
- Image tools often **garble lettering**, and badly garble Devanagari (the Nepali script). Keep words in English unless a Nepali speaker checks the result.
- **Don't show real students' faces.** If any of them are under 18, the ORION under-18 rules apply.
- **Prayer flags, stupas and the "Buddha eyes"** are religious symbols. They can appear respectfully in the landscape, but not as team logos or decoration on merch, unless the students themselves want it.

## The prompts

### 1. Key art: "Nepal Spark Academy" (poster, 4:5)
> A grand fantasy poster titled "NEPAL SPARK ACADEMY". A small hillside school built from Kathmandu-style red brick and carved wood, perched on green terraces with the snowy Himalayas behind at sunrise. Above the school, two great energy streams swirl in the sky: on the left an ice-blue and violet **lightning** current shaped like a crystal-glass dragon, on the right an ember-orange **fire** current shaped like a steel-armoured boar. Where they meet over the school roof they braid into one **green-gold** ribbon of light that pours down the hillside into the village below, lighting up homes, gardens and a water spring. Twenty-five small student silhouettes stand on the terrace looking up. Mood: hope, beginnings, "if we can do it, they can do it."

## Fill-in-the-blanks version (for the students to make their own)

> A [poster / badge / scene] for the [GLASS DRAGON / STEEL PIG] team of the Nepal Spark Academy, showing [what we are making] in [a place from our home: village, city street, school, mountain]. The [crystal dragon / steel boar] is [doing what]. The energy is [blue lightning for focus / orange fire for people] and it turns green-gold where it helps [who in our community]. Style: [our favourite art style]. Text: "[our words, in English]".

This is also a nice **first lesson**: the students write their own prompt, make the picture and explain it to the other team. That's English, art, AI tools and public speaking in one hour.
