# Free video editing for ORION, on a phone, with no budget 🎬📱

*Round 13, 6 October 2026. Beth: "I have no video editing capabilities. I would like to eventually … produce 3 to 5 minute video clips, if not 30-minute episodes … no money, no tools … Please do a deep search and give me a suggestion of the easiest way to do this, with a summary of each individual thing you present to me as well as a step-by-step guide" (doc line 398).*

**Before you start:**
- These tools are described from general knowledge, not a live search, and apps change their free tiers often. Check each app's current terms and price before relying on it.
- I can't make videos for you, but §4 gives search phrases for good video tutorials.
- None of these companies is endorsed; they're listed because they're widely used and have free options.

---

## 1. The short answer

**Start with your phone and one free editor.** For 3–5 minute clips:
- **iPhone:** iMovie (free, made by Apple).
- **Android, or any phone:** CapCut, or VN Video Editor (both have free tiers).
- **Images to video:** your Meta AI images can become a slideshow with motion (the "Ken Burns" pan and zoom) inside any of these apps.

**When you get a computer** (even a borrowed one): **DaVinci Resolve** (free version) is professional-grade, and is what you'd use for 30-minute episodes.

## 2. The tools, each in one paragraph

| Tool | Where | Cost | Good for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **iMovie** | iPhone, iPad, Mac | Free | The simplest start: trim, titles, music, transitions | Apple devices only; limited effects |
| **CapCut** | Phone, web, desktop | Free tier, plus paid "Pro" | Social-ready clips, auto-captions, templates, text-to-speech | Some features and exports moved behind the paid tier; read the privacy policy, as it's owned by ByteDance |
| **VN Video Editor** | Phone, desktop | Free tier | A multi-track timeline on a phone, no watermark (check current terms) | Fewer AI features |
| **Canva** (video) | Phone, web | Free tier | Turning images and text into short animated videos; good for promo cards | Long videos get clunky |
| **Clipchamp** | Web, Windows | Free tier | Browser editing with captions | Some features are paid |
| **DaVinci Resolve** | Windows, Mac, Linux, iPad | Free version | Full professional editing, colour, audio, long episodes | Needs a decent computer; has a learning curve |
| **Shotcut / Kdenlive** | Windows, Mac, Linux | Free, open source | Lighter computers; no accounts, no subscriptions | Less polished |
| **AI image-to-video tools** (e.g. Meta AI's "animate" button, which you already have) | Phone / web | Often free with limits | Making your still images move ("Animate the Leviathan…") | Short clips only; check the usage rights for public posting |

## 3. Step-by-step: your first ORION clip (3 minutes, phone only)

**Goal:** a 3-minute "Welcome to the ORION Engine" video from your Meta AI images, with your voice.

1. **Plan (10 minutes).** Write 8 short lines, one per title, using the X thread blurbs in `ORION_X_THREAD_BLURBS.md`. That's your script.
2. **Gather.** Save 8–12 images from your album to your phone, one per line plus an intro and an outro.
3. **Record your voice.** Open your phone's voice recorder and read the script slowly. Re-record any line you stumble on; separate files are fine.
4. **Open the editor** (iMovie or CapCut) → **New project** → add the images in order.
5. **Set timing.** Make each image about 15–20 seconds. Turn on pan and zoom ("Ken Burns" in iMovie; "Animation → Zoom" in CapCut) so it isn't static.
6. **Add your voice.** Import the recording(s) as audio and line them up with each image.
7. **Add titles.** Put each title on its image (ORION in capitals).
8. **Add captions.** In CapCut, use **Auto captions**; in iMovie, add text. Captions matter: most people watch with the sound off, and they're an accessibility must.
9. **Add music, quietly.** Use the app's free music library, or music you have rights to. Popular songs can get your video muted or taken down.
10. **Export** at 1080p. Vertical (9:16) suits X, TikTok and Reels; horizontal (16:9) suits YouTube.
11. **Credit.** End card: "Images: Beth with Meta AI. Music: [source]. THE ORION ENGINE GLOBAL PROJECT."

**Next steps up:**
- **5-minute episode:** add a "cold open" (one dramatic image and line), then three short segments.
- **30-minute episode:** move to DaVinci Resolve on a computer, and treat it as 6 five-minute segments stitched together.
