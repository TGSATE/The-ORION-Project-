## 1. The one-page pitch (attach to every proposal)

**THE ORION ENGINE GLOBAL PROJECT** is a free-to-play, layered online world for learning, creativity, community and play, all on one account:
- **The ORION Engine:** welcome page, tutorials, credits.
- **The Singularity:** your private home hub.
- **Elder's Garden:** the global ancestral tree, celebrations, and training grounds with mentors.
- **The Playground:** a social and creative community with no ads.
- **The ORION Chronicles:** an MMO with dragons, guilds and crafting.
- **The Ascent:** education at your own pace.
- **ORION's Gate:** global community projects, reviewed by councils.
- **UIAR:** an augmented-reality layer (a watch and an earpiece) that goes fully immersive only when the player is somewhere safe.

**Core principles:**
- capability never becomes authority automatically;
- child safety comes first;
- no permanent death;
- creators are always credited.

**Team:** Beth Jones (designer, former 8th-grade English teacher), with a volunteer development team (Mike, Matthew, Lucien, Ryan) and contributors.

**Stage:** design and art direction. Looking for collaborators and funding for a first prototype.

## 2. Dungeons & Dragons (Wizards of the Coast): the Path of the Game Master

**Ask:** a collaboration or licence so ORION's 18+ **Game Master training ground** can teach game-mastering using D&D, or a co-branded "learn to DM" path.
**What's in it for them:**
- new game masters trained inside a free world;
- a pathway from ORION into their tabletop products;
- a family-safe, teacher-designed onboarding.
**What ORION offers:**
- the Game Master's Field design (`GAME_MASTER_TRAINING_GROUND.md`);
- credit placement;
- a shared playtest.
**Fact to know:** Wizards released the D&D 5th-edition **System Reference Document 5.1** under a Creative Commons licence in 2023. Some game rules can be used under that licence's terms without a deal, but names, settings and trademarks still can't. Have a lawyer read the licence before relying on it.
