## 3. Gold Lock, final wording 💎

> A skill orb can be gold-locked only at level 100 while the Gold Krion meter is balanced. **Once locked, it stays locked forever.** The one exception is the player's own choice: they may redistribute that orb's KRION energy into other skill orbs **of the same skill set**. Nothing else unlocks it: not leaving Gold, not time passing, not other players.

What the computer checked:
- If a locked orb ever becomes unlocked, it was the player choosing to redistribute **that orb**.
- Over any run of events without that choice, a locked orb stays locked.
- Redistributing never creates or destroys KRION energy: the skill set's total is the same before and after.
- Orbs other than the two involved are untouched.

**One thing to look at** (not a contradiction): the Round 12 rule says locked progress never goes down. With redistribution, the *orb* goes down but the *skill set's total* doesn't. I've written it that way. Tell me if you see it differently.

Your sniper picture is recorded with the rule in `DIAMOND_RULES.md`: a master who is already balanced in meter, energy, intent and focus can go straight to a level 100 archery orb without the grind a beginner goes through.
