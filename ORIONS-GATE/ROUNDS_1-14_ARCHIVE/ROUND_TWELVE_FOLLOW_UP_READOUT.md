She taps it, it lifts out, and she keeps every bit of progress. Now a different case: Mara's school team ran a project through a council gauntlet. That record stays, because other people's work and votes rest on it. One exception always wins: if the bubble was **a private memory she chose to share**, it stays 100% hers, even if it later touched a gauntlet. (That last edge case is my reading of your sentence. Please confirm it, decision D-7.)

*Checked in Lean (`RequestProject/RoundTwelve/AlbumMathAndRulings.lean`):*
- the owner can always take back a private-origin bubble;
- the owner can take back any bubble that never went through a gauntlet;
- a non-private gauntlet record stays;
- only the owner can take a bubble back;
- taking it back removes it and costs no progress.

**The Playground with no billboards.** The Playground is loud on purpose, and it is where the tornadoes start. Nobody can loop spam or sell there. Next door, attached to it, floats the **Recruitment Skybox**: a gathering place where people from the socials arrive and get pulled into an adventure or into a project proof. *Open:* how to block ad loops technically (moderation, rate limits, a ban on repeated identical posts). That is for the coders.
