## 2. The Adult Penalty, explained plainly

**What it is.** It's an idea from *your own* Round One documents. The phrase is in *ORION's Gate MMO Round One Summary*, *Round One*, *The Ascent* and three drafts of *The Orion Chronicles*:

- Kids who grow up in the youth side of the game keep all their stats and history when they turn 18. They walk into the adult world already strong.
- Someone who creates a **brand-new account as an adult** starts at the tutorial levels. Adults tend to rush the tutorial, so they come out **weaker** than the graduating kids. The documents call that gap the "Adult Penalty".

**What I meant by "I corrected the earlier 'not a rule'":**

- In Round 9 I wrote: "the Adult Penalty isn't in anything you've shared, so it's recorded as **not an ORION rule**." That was wrong. The documents I hadn't yet read (they're nested several links deep) do contain it.
- So last round I *fixed my own mistake*: the wiki now says "this comes from your design text". My earlier statement didn't contradict your design; it contradicted the facts, and I've corrected it.
- "Whether to keep it is your call" just means: now that you know where it came from, you decide if it stays in the game.

**The one real question left (register C-04).** Your documents say it two ways:
- "the bridge **imposes** an Adult Penalty" — the game *adds* a penalty to new adult accounts;
- adults "**naturally** try to blast through the tutorial … **resulting in** them entering … weaker" — no extra rule; they're simply behind because they skipped the learning.

**Which one do you mean?** If it's the second, nothing needs to be coded: it happens by itself.
