# Council starter cards: one per seat 🌀🃏 (OR20.4, 11 October 2026)

*Beth, OR20.3: "you already have all of this information if you just do a deep dive." She was right. The seats were already written down in `COUNCIL_RELAY_EXAMINATION.md` (the full relay lap from 10 October) and in `ORION_SUMMARY_CURRENT.md` (council rules). This page turns them into starter cards. Nothing here is new, and no seat changed.*

**How to use a card:** open that AI's app, paste the card's "Paste this in" block as the first message, then paste your Doc link or your latest readout. Each card tells that AI its job, its limits, and where the shared memory lives, so you don't have to re-explain it every time.

**The shared memory for every seat** (one front door): `START_HERE.md`, and for AIs, `ORION_HANDOFF_FOR_UIARS.md`. Until the library is somewhere public (see the OR20.4 readout, path C), paste the handoff text in yourself.

**Council rules, as already locked** (`ORION_SUMMARY_CURRENT.md`): yes/no votes with no abstentions; consensus is 4 out of 5; a personal council has at least 5 seats. Any "no" triggers a reassessment. **No AI holds authority to change a rule.** Seats are jobs, so any AI can be swapped out.
