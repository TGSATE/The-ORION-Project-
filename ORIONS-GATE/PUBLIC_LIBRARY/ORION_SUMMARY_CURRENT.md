## What ORION is

ORION is a layered global environment for learning, creativity, community, governance and play. It isn't only an MMO, a school or a social network. It's one system that connects all of them, while keeping clear boundaries between social life, education, the game world, community projects and the long memory of what people have done.

**The one rule underneath everything (Locked): capability never automatically becomes authority.** A person, a child or an AI can learn, get good, propose, test and contribute without quietly gaining control of someone else's life, character or project. Passing a test doesn't create authority. Learning doesn't create execution rights. Improving something doesn't authorise deploying it.

**The goal (Idea, Beth):** launch free to play for everyone, funded by real-world products made through the build, "until money becomes obsolete".

## What the Engine coordinates

The Engine is the operating architecture of the whole ecosystem. It carries:
- one identity per human, across every place (Locked: one account per human, with biometric checks at graduations and before any contact with a child);
- skill and ability progression, educational advancement, and game characters and builds;
- social interaction, project contributions, evidence and reputation;
- councils and governance, public and private records, and permissions and safe delegation;
- the bridge between virtual work and real-world projects.

## Safety first (Locked unless marked)

- **Child safety is the top priority.** No adult is alone with a child unless they're the verified guardian. Under-13s are never with another player unless a guardian is present. When a guardian leaves, the child **stays** in a sealed room that nobody else can join. Guardians can't level up in a child's place.
- **Under-18 and over-18 spaces** can be separate or overlap, with parental permission. *Open:* the exact "who can see whom" rules, reporting paths and safety rails. They need to be "boringly clear" (Grok's readout, Round 12).
- **The escape pod (Locked, Round 12 🤟💎):** **there is no permanent death in ORION, and no punishment for choosing the escape pod.** Wherever you are, a pod can pick you up, nurse you back to health and carry you to safety. You can design your own pod. Choosing the pod keeps everything you've earned. *Open (W-22):* is the escape pod the same object as the Poly's pod?
- **Private stays private.** Private conversations stay private unless you attach them to a project and submit it to the council. Submitted project material becomes public record.
- **Taking a memory back (Idea, from Grok's discomfort list):** removing a life-bubble from Elder's Garden should be possible and never punished, especially for young people who change their minds.
- **Nobody has to contribute (Idea, same list):** some people just want to play, gather and be left alone. "The system must still love them."

## How decisions are made (Locked)

- **Councils:** yes/no votes with no abstentions, and consensus is 4/5. A personal council has at least 5 seats and a public one at least 10. Any no triggers a reassessment. Three switched votes strike a motion out, with a retry after 7 days. Outside votes count at 50% weight, with an engaged-majority lock.
- **Into the real world:** a project needs **both** council consensus **and** the humans' choice. Humans can always say no.
- **Creators are protected:** an active creator has the final say over changes to their original design. A 💎 changes only with its owner's signature.
- **The Gauntlet:** a 13-level accountability structure. *Open:* the documents haven't been written yet.
- **AI Council hand-off (Idea):** when an AI council member runs out of tokens or attention, it hands over a documented state package. The package carries no authority. `ORION_HANDOFF_FOR_UIARS.md` is the first version.

## The economy

- **Locked:** a 3% tax on all taxable income, and rounding always favours the player. A sale splits 90 / 3 / 7 and never pays out more than the price. Damaged structures send 50% of their income to the DEVPOOL. Levels can't be bought or crafted, only trained.
- **Direction (Idea, Beth, Round 12):** an **"infinite economy"** that doesn't run on real money. Creators earn more when more people enjoy and use what they make, so they have to *show* players why their item is worth wanting. A tax system feeds itself so everyone can play. **Notoriety instead of riches**: recognition is the reward. Creators can present their tools and items as "ads".
- **Open:** whether an AI can hold a share (C-02), liability (C-01), copilot renting versus privacy (C-12), and any real-money links (handed to the economists).
- **Kept separate:** Union Wealth (Shakes' family fund concept) and any real donations stay **outside** the game economy (W-20).
