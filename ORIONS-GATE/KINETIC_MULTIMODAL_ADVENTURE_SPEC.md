# Choose your own adventure in every mode: audio, video, text and *kinetic* 🎙️🎥✍️🏗️

*R16·L7, 8 October 2026. Beth (🌀 on the Ghost Writer packet): "is there any way we could set up coding for applications that include audio, video and text and kinetic processing of the information so they can truly choose their own adventure and how they want to interact. Kinetic meaning, like, go do something to learn skills… go out, do the shit, prove you know how."*

**Short answer: yes, it can be built.** Nothing here is built yet. This page is the design a builder can start from. The phone-only version (§4) works today with free tools.

## 1. The story first: how a player meets it

Mara opens the Ghost Writer and taps the mic instead of typing. She talks for twelve minutes about wanting to build things with her hands. The ghost writer gives her the whole thing back organized, nothing cut, plus a background check: "Here's what public sources say about getting started in carpentry."

Her paths appear:

- 📖 **Read** a summary.
- 🎧 **Listen** to it read aloud.
- 🎥 **Watch** a short how-to video.
- 🏗️ **Go do it:** "Build a simple sawhorse this week. Bring back a photo or a 30-second video, and tell the story of what went wrong."

She picks 🏗️. Four days later she uploads a wobbly sawhorse and a voice note. The ghost writer weaves it into her next chapter. A mentor in the Guild leaves a 🤟 and one tip about bracing. Her **Carpentry I** skill lights up as *evidence submitted, mentor-checked*. That isn't a licence. It's a real, visible step on her sheet.

## 2. The four ways in, and the four ways out

| Mode | Player gives (in) | Ghost writer returns (out) |
|---|---|---|
| ✍️ Text | typing, pasted notes | the organized chapter, summaries, paths |
| 🎙️ Audio | voice notes, dictation | a transcript first (the player can correct it), then the chapter; optional read-aloud |
| 🎥 Video | a clip of something they did or made | a written description the player confirms, then the chapter |
| 🏗️ Kinetic | **doing a real task in the world**, then bringing back evidence | a skill step on their sheet, plus the next challenge |

The player picks the mode **every level**, and can switch at any time. Nobody is forced into video or voice.

## 3. How a kinetic quest works

1. **Quest card:** what to do, why it matters, how long, what tools, the safety notes, and what counts as evidence.
2. **Go do it** (offline).
3. **Bring back evidence:** a photo, a short video, a voice note, a finished file, or a mentor's sign-off.
4. **Check:** a human mentor or council member confirms it. The AI can describe and organize the evidence, but **it never approves its own quest** (UIAR rule).
5. **Skill step:** the skill moves to *attempted*, then *evidence submitted*, then *mentor-checked*, then *certified elsewhere* (for example a recognized course). ORION never claims a step it can't back up.

**Construction crew idea (Beth's note about Elon):** a "prove you know how" track could let someone show real, checked work to anyone hiring a crew. That's an ORION idea, not a partnership. No one at any company has been contacted. Real trades jobs also need recognized safety training and, in many places, licences. The quest track can point to those; it can't replace them.

## 4. What works today on a phone, for free

| Step | Free tool (check current terms yourself) | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Talk instead of type | the phone keyboard's mic, or the voice mode in a free AI chat | free |
| Have it read aloud | the phone's built-in "Speak screen" / "Select to speak" | free |
| Learn by watching | YouTube, freeCodeCamp, Khan Academy | free |
| Bring back evidence | the phone camera; keep originals in your own Drive | free (storage limits apply) |
| Mentor check | a reply in the team chat or Doc with 🤟 | free |

## 5. What a builder would add later

1. Speech-to-text with an edit-before-use screen.
2. Video upload that keeps the original under the player's control. Faces and voices of other people need their consent.
3. A **quest card library** by skill, written and safety-reviewed by people who actually do that skill.
4. An **evidence ledger** on the character sheet: what was submitted, who checked it, when.
5. Mentor tools: a 🤟 button, one-tip comments, and "needs another try" with no penalty.

## 6. How it fits the existing build

**Fits:** the Training Ground and the Proofs Arena (practice, then show the work); Guild & Union skills; the character sheet's auto-fill; the Reality Anchor rules (real world first; say when the story is a story); the escape pod rule (no punishment for stopping); "capability never automatically becomes authority" (a skill step isn't a job title).

**Clashes / needs a ruling:**

- **Minors:** no hazardous kinetic quests (power tools, roofs, electrical work) for under-18 players. Guardian approval for any offline quest. Never ask a child for location or face video. This follows the existing under-18 structure.
- **Safety of the quest itself:** a person must review every quest card before it goes live, and every card must say "stop if it feels unsafe."
- **Privacy:** evidence is private by default. The player chooses to share it.
- **Not a credential:** "mentor-checked" must never be shown as a licence or certification.
