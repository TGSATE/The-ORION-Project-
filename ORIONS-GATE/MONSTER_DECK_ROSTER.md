## 4. The deck as dice 🎲

Your 😈 idea: in the Training Ground's monster adventure, **drawing a monster card is the dice roll.**
- A **d20 deck** is 20 cards, numbered 1 to 20 (the "Roll number" on each card). Shuffle and draw: the number is your roll, and the monster on it is who shows up.
- Put the card back and reshuffle before the next roll, so every roll is fresh.
- **Checked in Lean** (`RequestProject/R17/Level6Checks.lean`, part 4): a deck with one card per face, however it's shuffled, holds every face exactly once. So a fair shuffle makes a fair roll.
- The same goes for your mystic cards on the Rat Slap deck: the art never changes the rules (checked at 17.5).
