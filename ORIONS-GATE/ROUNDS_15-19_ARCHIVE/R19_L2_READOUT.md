## 1. Why your Google Photos didn't show up 📷

I checked the Doc two ways: the plain text, and the full-format copy that includes pictures. **Neither contains a single image.** So the photos never made it into the Doc. It isn't that I skipped them.

**The usual reasons on a phone:**
1. In the Google Docs app on iPhone, **Insert → Image → From photos** only offers your *phone's* camera roll. Google Photos pictures have to be **saved to the phone first** (open the picture in Google Photos → Share → Save to device), then inserted.
2. Pasting a Google Photos **share link** gives you text (a link), not a picture. And "insert here" typed into the Doc doesn't pull anything in.
3. Big uploads on a weak signal sometimes silently fail. The picture shows a grey box, then vanishes.

**The easy fix for me:** upload the pictures (or the spacer screenshot) **as files** when you send the Doc link, or put them in a Google Drive folder shared as "anyone with the link". Either way I can open them directly.
