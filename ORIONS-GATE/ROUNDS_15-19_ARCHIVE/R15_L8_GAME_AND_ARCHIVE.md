## The armor choice and the first playable tutorial slice

**Your correction:** at the chest, the *trade-in* means a **higher set of armor**, contrasted with taking your chances with the dragon for **possible better armor and additional tools**. This is not the same as a guaranteed starter pack in the 15.7 wording. `GAME_LORE_CODEX.md` preserves both descriptions and flags the unresolved bundle. **Please choose:** (a) Does the safe trade-in also grant the starter orb and pack, or does it replace them? (b) Can a trained player elect to fight the dragon? (c) On losing, what is retained, and can a younger player skip the fight? In a prototype display the two choices and their outcomes **before** commitment. Do not secretly punish a player for failing the dragon; preserve the never-stuck rule.

**Plexus / the P-word:** *Poly Pod Plexus* is my favorite of the candidate names because a plexus is a connected network, not just a collection. It is also less “egg-only” than *Poly Pod Pack*. Other options: *Poly Pod Patch* (garden plot, friendly for young players); *Poly Pod Parade* (festival/opening, lighthearted); *Poly Pod Port* (transfer point between companions and the MMO). **Plexus is an opinion, not a lock**; keep “Clutch” as a searchable alias until you choose.

**Morning Fly / Marty McFly idea:** start a one-handed, low-speed *Fairy Garden driving tutorial*: choose “guided” or “experiment”; learn brake, turn, look and pull over with optional captions/audio; enter a morphing flower path; hitting an object playfully releases an **imaginary** creature to meet, not harm, and pauses the driving lesson. A forgiving rewind/respawn handles collisions; reduced-motion and non-timed options are available. Keep driving safety skills separate from vehicular fantasy and avoid treating it as a real-world driver certification.

**Tarot/crystal mini-game, based on the newly readable 26 January `Crystal/Tarot` document:** a 5-slot spread the player can rearrange: top-left **Son of Swords**, top-middle **Seven of Pentacles**, top-right **Eight of Wands** (the document explicitly corrects an earlier Tower misread); bottom-left **Tarantula** (Wild Unknown Animal Spirit), bottom-right **Ten of Wands** (Gilded Tarot Royale). Players choose what a card means *for their story*, with an optional deck-specific interpretation panel and a “not a prediction” switch. Train on **sequence** (notice → reflect → act → check load), not a supernatural result or health claim. The pictured left-hand crystals (crazy-lace agate, tiger's eye) and right-hand mahogany obsidian can be movable art tokens; a separate science station can measure piezoelectric voltage with real sensor hardware, not assign healing powers to those stones. A card deck's distinctive images/text need a separate permission check before reproducing them; use **ORION-original illustrations and freshly written descriptions** in the playable version. Offer reversible moves, keyboard/one-hand support and age-appropriate prompts. The January document itself is a compiled AI interpretation, not an authenticated inventory of the original deck photos; ask for deck names/photos if accuracy matters.

**Non-monetized collaboration loop:** quests award optional *credits and access to creative tools*, never tradeable cash/token rewards or paid advantage; no paid random draws, no referral payout and no speculative-token tie-in. Make contributions opt-in, allow contributors to see credits and withdraw future use where feasible, keep minors' submissions moderated and don't make spending or recruiting a requirement. This is design direction, not a deployed safeguard.
