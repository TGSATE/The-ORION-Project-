**Jev in plain words.** Jev isn't a chatbot and doesn't write paragraphs. You give it some text plus a question with fixed answers, and it returns the answer as data, with a confidence. It has three question types:
- yes/no ("noul");
- pick one from a list ("choice");
- a score on a scale.

Programs use it as a fast, cheap judge: "is this request clear?", "does this answer match the data?", "how urgent, 1–10?". That's why the posts pair it with a writer model. Mike has a copy of a small community command-line tool for it (`jev-cli`, unofficial).
