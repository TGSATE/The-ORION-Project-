## 11. Calculus for a pattern brain
Calculus *is* patterns. Learn it by picture first and symbols second: a derivative is "how steep, right here", an integral is "add up the thin slices". Watch the shapes before the rules. Then play with patterns: the slope of x² is 2x, of x³ is 3x², and you'll spot the rule yourself. A free visual video series or Khan Academy suits this style. **Voynich:** yes, there's crossover. Statisticians and mathematicians do study the manuscript's word patterns. Their tools (word frequencies, information measures) are counting and probability rather than calculus.
