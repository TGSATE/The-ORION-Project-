### The white-hole breadcrumb trail: claim → status

1. **Black holes:** observed indirectly in many ways, including gravitational effects and images of their surroundings; [NASA's black-hole overview](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/black-holes/) describes what is established and says black holes are **not wormholes** or portals.
2. **White holes:** a time-reversed black-hole-like solution in idealized general-relativity mathematics, considered in theoretical discussions. The X post says none has been found. It provides no telescope detection, measurement or predictive test of an astrophysical white hole. “Scientists believe” is too broad if it implies an observation or consensus on existence.
3. **Wormhole links:** hypothetical spacetime constructions, not demonstrated travel tunnels. No evidence in the post establishes that a real black hole has a traversable link to a real white hole. NASA's black-hole overview specifically cautions against describing black holes as wormholes.
4. **Big Bang = white hole:** an analogy/speculative proposal, not an established description of the early universe. The post's “Source NASA. Black Holes and Wormholes” is not a precise citation to evidence for white holes. Ask for a specific publication, model assumptions, predicted measurable signature and actual observation before treating a breadcrumb as confirmation.
5. **Your 13-layer toroidal / singularity-to-avatar picture:** a *creative model to articulate*, not refuted or confirmed by this post. To assess a physical claim, label its quantities, give equations and distinct predictions that measurements could distinguish from current models.
