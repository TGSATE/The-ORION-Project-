## 4. Your note about a lawyer ⚖️

You wrote that if ORION takes off you'd like to find an attorney, because there's a big corporation and a hospital you'd like to sue. **I can't give legal advice.** Legal claims have deadlines that depend on where you live and what happened, so if this matters to you, talk to a qualified attorney sooner rather than later, whether or not the game takes off. In the US, your state bar's **lawyer referral service** and local **legal aid** offices are the usual starting points, and many attorneys offer a first consultation. Keep it separate from the game and from public posts until a lawyer has advised you.
