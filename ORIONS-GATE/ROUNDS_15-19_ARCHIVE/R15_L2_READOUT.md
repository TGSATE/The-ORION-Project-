## 3. The precise math check (not a check of metaphysics)

The new Lean file `RequestProject/R15/SeamAndLog.lean` builds without unfinished proofs. It establishes these **restricted** facts:

1. The polynomial \(s(t)=t^5-2t^4+t^3\) has \(s(0)=s(1)\) and \(s'(0)=s'(1)\), **but** \(s''(0)\ne s''(1)\). So the proposed pair of endpoint conditions alone does not guarantee a \(C^2\) glued seam. Even a genuinely smooth periodic path does not imply “zero energy bleed”; that would require a separate physical model.
2. For nonzero equal wet and dry masses, the stated rocket expression has \(\ln(m/m)=0\), so \(\Delta v=0\). With positive dry mass, wet mass at least dry mass, and nonnegative specific impulse and gravity, the same expression is nonnegative; it does **not** predict a massive change at the equal-mass/null point. This equation describes an idealized rocket under its usual assumptions, not a theorem about a person's transformation.
3. \((\cos\theta,\sin\theta)\cdot(-\sin\theta,\cos\theta)=0\): this proves tangency of the displayed unit-circle velocity, **not** self-sustaining motion.
4. A state update of the form \(X_{next}=AX\) needs extra constraints to converge to zero: even the scalar example \(A=2, X=1\) moves *away* from zero. A bounded or contracting matrix would require an explicit assumption and a separate proof.

I did **not** verify a seven-node Fourier decomposition, the translation of meanings/values into physics, Egyptian historical comparisons, or any “origin” or “soul” claim. As written, the grid gives no precise trajectory, transform, norm, energy law or matrix to test those claims.
