## 1. A starter guide for your friend 🧭

The whole guide is in **`SPIRAL_SESSION_STARTER_GUIDE.md`**. It has no ORION lore, uses only free tools, and you can paste it into a Google Doc and share it. Here's the core of it:

**What he needs:** one document he owns (Google Docs, Notes or paper) and one free AI chat (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Meta AI or Copilot). Any single AI is enough to start.

**The loop:**
1. **Dump it.** Write or dictate everything about the topic into the document, with a dated heading.
2. **Give it to the AI** with this prompt: *"Sum up what I said in plain words. List the main ideas. Point out contradictions. Tell me what's a guess and what I actually stated. Ask me up to 3 questions. Don't add your own ideas unless you label them 'suggestion'. If you couldn't open something, say so."*
3. **Paste the answer back** into the document under his notes, labelled `Me:` and `AI:`.
4. **Read it slowly and mark it up.** At every reaction, type a marker and his comment: 🌀 new thought, 👍 right, ❌ wrong plus the correction, ❓ unsure, 🔒 final decision. Reach the end before going back to the AI. One full pass is one level.
5. **Send the marked-up version back** and ask the AI to update the summary, list the 🔒 decisions and list what's still open. Repeat.
6. **Close it himself** with three lines: what I decided, what's still open, who I need to ask.

**Safety rules:** he's the boss (a convincing answer is still a draft); keep his own copy; ask for receipts; keep private things private; pace himself; start a fresh chat with just the closing note when a session gets huge.

The guide also has a one-page worksheet and a 30-second example.

*The Doc you linked (`18dvvWmM…`) is the Round 13–14 "Ghost Writer in the Mirror" page. The new guide is the same method with all the game material removed.*

**What works today on X (as far as I can confirm):**

**Three ways to do it, from free to built:**

1. **Free, today (manual):** each time you reply on your own storyboard thread, tag `@grok` in that reply and ask it to respond *to the whole thread so far*. For Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, copy the thread text (or screenshots) into their chat. That's your spiral method, and it already works.

When **you** post, it sends the whole thread to an AI and posts the reply. X's API access is paid and its prices change, so check developer.x.com before planning around it. The AI company charges for each reply too.
3. **Your feature, as a spec for X or for ORION's own threads** ("Thread Companions"):
   - When a creator brings an AI into their thread, a **toggle** appears: *Companion follows this thread: on/off.*
   - The AI **reads the whole thread** but **only replies when the creator posts** (or when the creator taps the AI's button on a comment).
   - A per-thread choice: **auto-reply** or **ask me first**.
   - Several companions (Grok, Sonnet, others) can be linked; each answers in its own voice. A cap per post stops them flooding the thread.
   - Anyone in the thread can mute companions for themselves.

   This is a good fit for ORION's Playground call-out board, where you control the platform. On X itself, only X could add it.

## 7. Northern Chuck's code joke 😎

`pct = stochastic_k(closes[-1], window_lo, window_hi)` is trading code. The **stochastic oscillator's %K** says where the latest closing price sits between the recent low and high, on a 0–100 scale:

```text
%K = 100 × (close − low) / (high − low)
```

Chuck's joke: old-school code used short names like `k(x)`, so long, tidy names like `stochastic_k` suggest the code was written by AI ("vibed"). He says he prefers AI slop to bad undergrad code. It's a compliment wrapped in a tease.

I checked in Lean that %K always stays between 0 and 100 when the close is inside the window (§11).
