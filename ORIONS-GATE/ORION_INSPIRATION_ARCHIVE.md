# 🎨 ORION Inspiration Archive (founders + DEV admin team)

*Started OR20.6, 12 October 2026, from Beth's ask: "all of the artwork that is attached to the posts that we are able to capture and grab are added into an inspirational type archive for the founders and DEV's admin team… I don't want to steal their artwork… we can utilize our builds and compile as long as we give them their credit, inspired by kind of thing."*

## How this archive works

- **Every picture stays linked to its maker.** Each entry keeps the post link, the picture links and a ready-made credit line. The pictures stay where their makers posted them. I don't copy them into the folder, so the credit can never get separated from the art.
- **Use it as a mood board.** Open a picture, then make your own art *inspired by* it: your characters, your colours, your words. Credit line to use: **"Inspired by @handle (link)"**.
- **Copying is different from being inspired.** Posting something publicly doesn't give everyone permission to reuse that exact picture. If a DEV wants to put someone's actual picture into the game, a post or anything sold, message the maker and ask first. A one-line ask is in the next section. (I can't give legal advice; this is just the safe habit.)
- **The poster isn't always the maker.** Some accounts repost other people's art. Where I could see who really made it (a watermark, a famous telescope photo), the entry says so. Credit the maker.
- **🎨 = art or diagram you can draw on. ℹ️ = info post** (screenshots, lists, news). Info posts stay listed so nothing you grabbed gets lost.
- **New posts get added every level** from the X links you drop in the Doc. Already filed = not repeated.

## Ask-first message (copy, paste, change the name)

```text
Hi @handle! Love this piece: [post link]. We're building ORION, a community game project. Could we use it (with credit to you) in [where]? Totally fine if not; we'll keep it as inspiration only and make our own art.
```
