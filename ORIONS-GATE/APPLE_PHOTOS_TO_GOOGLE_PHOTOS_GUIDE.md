# Getting your iPhone photos into Google Photos, with no Apple payments 📸

*Written 9 October 2026 (Council of War session). Beth asked for "a how-to guide on how to connect my Apple Photos to my Google Photos". She wanted no more paying Apple for storage, and no more being asked to pick photos one at a time.*

There are **two separate jobs**. Do them in this order.

---

## Job 1: new photos go to Google Photos automatically

1. Install **Google Photos** from the App Store. It's free, so sign in with your Google account.
2. In Google Photos, tap your **profile picture** (top right), then **Photos settings**, then **Backup**. Turn **Backup** on.
3. **This step stops the "choose photos" prompts.** Open the iPhone **Settings** app, then **Privacy & Security**, then **Photos**, then **Google Photos**, and pick **Full Access**.
   - The prompts come from **Limited Access**. That setting makes you pick photos by hand, or from albums you never set up. With Full Access, Google Photos can see everything, and you don't have to make collections.
4. Open Google Photos once with Wi-Fi on and leave it open for a while. The first upload can take hours if you have lots of pictures.
5. Optional: under **Backup**, then **Backup quality**, pick **Storage saver** if you want to stretch the free space.

## Job 2: the old photos already stored in iCloud

Apple offers a free tool that **copies** your iCloud photos straight into Google Photos. You don't have to download them first.

1. Go to **privacy.apple.com** in a browser and sign in with your Apple ID.
2. Choose **Transfer a copy of your data**, then **iCloud Photos**, then **Google Photos**.
3. Sign in to Google when it asks. Apple emails you when the copy is finished, which can take a few days.
4. It's a copy, so nothing is deleted from Apple.

## Then (and only then) stop paying Apple

1. **Check first.** Open photos.google.com on any browser and make sure your pictures are there.
2. **Watch out for this.** If iCloud Photos had **Optimize iPhone Storage** on, your phone may only hold small versions, and the full-size originals live only in iCloud. Wait until the Job 2 copy is done before you turn anything off.
3. Turn off **iCloud Photos**: open **Settings**, tap **your name**, then **iCloud**, then **Photos**, and switch it off.
4. Cancel the paid plan: open **Settings**, tap **your name**, then **iCloud**, then **Manage Account Storage** (or **Manage Storage**), then **Change Storage Plan**, then **Downgrade Options**, and pick **Free (5 GB)**.

## What this costs (honest version)

| What | Cost |
|---|---|
| Google Photos app and backup | Free, up to **15 GB**. Gmail and Drive share that 15 GB. |
| More Google space (Google One) | Paid, if you go over 15 GB. Google sets the price and it changes, so check in the app. |
| Apple's transfer tool | Free |
| iCloud after you downgrade | Free (5 GB) |

**So:** "no Apple payments" is completely doable. "No payments at all" depends on how many photos you have. If you already have more than 15 GB, you'd swap an Apple bill for a Google one unless you thin the library out.

*Menu names change a little between iPhone versions. If a step doesn't match your screen, the words to search for in Settings are "Photos", "Full Access" and "Downgrade". I couldn't test this on your phone.*
