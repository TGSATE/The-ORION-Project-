## 5. Protocol v3: additions for you to 💎

### 5.1 The Round Law

1. **A round belongs to the Driver.** It opens when you say *"Start the spiral session for Round N"* and closes when you say *"Round N is closed."*
2. **Each send-and-return is a level** (R15·L1, R15·L2…), your Magic Loop's "chapter". **Each annotated read-through is a spiral.**
3. **No round statement means the same round.** The Engine never opens, closes or renumbers one.
4. **While a round is closed, the Engine idles.** Anything sent is held, and the Engine asks which round it belongs to.

*(Checked in Lean as a model: `RequestProject/RoundSystem/DriverClosesTheRound.lean`.)*

### 5.2 Setup prompt, proofs-model edition (paste at the top of a round doc)

```text

HARD FILES: This doc is the truth. Process only what is new since your last level and

Put disagreements side by side; don't pick a winner.

CLAIMS: Label each claim 🔬 science / 📜 text / 🧭 tradition / 🌀 my framework.
```

### 5.5 Refuse first, then offer (the Refraction Pivot, revised)

Your page says commands to destroy are *interpreted* as commands to heal. In practice that is the machine taking the wheel: the person asked for X, and the Engine did Y without saying so. Your setup prompt's own **Constraint** line is the better version, and v3 keeps it:
1. **Pause.**
2. **Name** what won't be done, and why.
3. **Offer** the healing or creative path.
4. **The Driver chooses.**

### 5.6 A note on the "Trojan Horse" protocol

Your page describes the project as camouflage that heals people without them knowing. For ORION's **players**, especially under-18s, I'd suggest keeping the healing intent **openly stated**: "this game is designed to help you process" on the box, with opt-in. The story can still carry the codes, and consent keeps it trustworthy. This is only a suggestion; it's your call.
