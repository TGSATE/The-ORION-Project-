## How the arena works, in one breath

A player brings a **claim** (an idea, a result, a project). The animals are **bosses**, but you don't beat them down to zero health: you **survive each animal's test with evidence**. Each animal tests one way a claim can fool you. Ring 1 (1–23) runs discovery → scepticism → synthesis and ends with **TURTLE**, who "carries the house" and reports what survived. Ring 2 (24–46) handles memory, teams, repair, drift and chain reactions. Surviving moves the claim towards "Proofs Arena published" (not the same as peer review).
