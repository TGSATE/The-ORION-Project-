# ORION storyline library (candidates)

Beth's request (page v1559, re-confirmed in v1843): narrative links can feed **mini-game design and MMO lore for the different zones**. Where a link holds a complete storyline, record it here with:
- a short summary;
- the genre or gameplay that would fit;
- the creator, and whether they have given permission.

**With the creator's permission**, the stories can then be loaded into the launch library.

**Rules for this file:**
- A summary is written in our own words. We don't copy the creator's prose.
- **Nothing is loaded into the launch library until the permission column says "granted"**, with the date and where it was given.
- Lore stays lore. It is never presented as a scientific claim.
