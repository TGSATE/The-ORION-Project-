## C. The written guide (copy and paste into your own Doc) ✍️

> **HOW MY SPIRAL SESSIONS ARE BUILT (use it, change it, or ignore it)**
>
> This is how Beth runs a session with her AIs. It's the long, hard way: it slows you down so you actually look at things, and that's where the magic comes from. If your brain works differently, skip it. Nothing breaks.
>
> **The trunk (one per level):**
> 1. **NEW NEWS:** paste links, group-chat grabs and anything you found. If it's already in your notes, don't paste it twice.
> 2. **Start line:** `🌀🌀🌀🌀🌀🌀🌀🌀OR19.3`. OR = round, the number after the dot = level. Your spiral starts under it.
> 3. **Spiral zone:** talk, paste, dictate. Typos are fine. Put your own icon at the front of the lines you write if several people share the Doc.
> 4. **Readout examination:** when your AI answers, paste its reply under `🌀🌀🌀🌀🌀🌀🌀🌀OR19.3🧿` and mark it up:
>    - 👍 yes
>    - ❌ change this
>    - 🤦‍♀️ you missed me
>    - 🤟 love it
>    - 💎 locked, final
> 5. **Next level:** `🌀🌀🌀🌀🌀🌀🌀🌀⏭️OR19.4` means the next level starts here.
> 6. **NEW NEWS footer:** a "NEW NEWS" line at the bottom. Drop anything you find mid-spiral there instead of in the middle of your thoughts.
> 7. **End line:** `☢️☢️☢️🧿☢️☢️☢️` "Nothing left to see here". Your AI stops reading there.
>
> **Extras, if you like them:**
> - **🧿 at the end of a line:** "read this one closely".
> - **⏬ row … 🆙 row:** scratch space the AI skips (always close it with 🆙).
> - **A long emoji row plus blank lines:** "I stepped away here". A bookmark for you, not a separator.
> - **Round finished:** `⏭️OR20` on a separator means a new round starts.
>
> **Your first session (20 minutes):**
> 1. Copy the trunk.
> 2. Fill in NEW NEWS.
> 3. Spiral for 10 minutes without editing.
> 4. Send it to your AI with: *"Read my separators. Tell me which level I'm on and which I'm porting to, then answer everything I asked, in order, at the top."*
> 5. Paste the reply under 🧿 and mark it up.
> 6. Start the next level.
