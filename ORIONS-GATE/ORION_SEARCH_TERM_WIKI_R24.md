*7 October 2026. You asked (line 973) for "an exhaustive reputable search against all scientific, theologic, metaphysical… fields for any notable search terms" in your Gemini titles and summaries, with a wiki entry for each. Below are the notable terms, grouped by field.*

**Each entry has three parts:**
- **What it is**, in mainstream terms.
- **Status**: one of four labels:
  - 🔬 *established science*;
  - 📜 *text or history* (what a source says, not whether it is true);
  - 🧭 *tradition / belief* (a practice or belief system, not tested science);
  - 🌀 *your framework* (an ORION/TGS:ATE idea).
- **ORION use**: how it could serve the game.

**Caveats:**
- These are **reference summaries written from established sources, not new research**. Where I name a source, it's a standard reference you can look up.
- Where a claim is contested or unsupported, I say so plainly. That isn't a judgement on your use of it as lore.
- Personal, medical and legal titles are deliberately left out.
