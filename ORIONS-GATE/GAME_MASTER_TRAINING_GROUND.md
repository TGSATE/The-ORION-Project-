```
THE ORION ENGINE (welcome) → THE SINGULARITY (your pod) → ELDER'S GARDEN
                                                          ├─ Ren Fair commons (mentors, vendors, Saturn's Fair)
                                                          └─ TRAINING GROUNDS (one per team / member / storyline)
                                                                 ↓

                                                                 ↓
                                                ASCENT ACADEMY  or  CHRONICLES TRAINING
                                                                 ↓
                                                           ORION'S GATE (Gauntlet)
```

**Rules for every ground (drawn from locked rules):**
- **Safe failure:** the escape pod works everywhere. No permanent death, no punishment.
- **Fair quests:** no stage needs a skill you had no way to learn earlier.
- **Capability ≠ authority:** graduating gives you the skill and a badge, never power over other players.
- **Under-18:** a parent can open a private instance. Adult-only grounds (like the Game Master's Field) are 18+ and invisible to minors.
- **Aesthetics:** each ground keeps its team's look. When players reach the Playground they can keep that look or switch to the global one (line 174).

## 4. The Lost in the Shadows decoding line (Ascent and Classroom station)

> "My poetry book *Lost in the Shadows* can be garbled, filtered, and part of the decoding translation skill line in the training ground, just to give people a chance to not walk in with completely garbled language." (line 368)

**How it works:**
1. **Garble levels 5 → 0.** A poem is shown at garble level 5 (letters shuffled, words in a cipher, emoji substitutions). Each solved clue lowers the level. At level 0 the poem reads clearly.
2. **Skills earned:** pattern matching, context guessing, emoji-to-meaning, and cipher basics. These feed the Poly's language progress: decoding practice makes foreign speech less garbled later.
3. **Safety text is never garbled** (💎). The game only garbles the poems.
4. **Credit:** Beth is credited as the author. The poems stay her property, and she chooses which ones go in.

**What I need from Beth:** the poems, or a choice of 3–5 to start with. I haven't seen the book's text, so none of it is quoted here.
