## 1. What a Dragon Hunt is

The **Flying Dragon Team (FDT)**, the referees of the dragon wars, run the hunts. Any player can **call a hunt**. The caller lays **four Monster Deck cards** on the roulette board. Each player then **picks their card** or **gets assigned one**. Then the dragons go out after their monster *inside the game*.

**Ground rule (part of the game, not a footnote):** hunts target **Monster Deck cards only**. These are invented monsters such as Plaid Ripper, the jesters and the clown car baddies. **A hunt card never stands for a real person.** No real names, photos, addresses or accounts go on a card or into marching orders. If something real is going on, players use real-world help (§6), not a hunt. The FDT referees call a foul on any card that breaks this rule and pull it from the board.

## 2. How a hunt runs (the roulette)

1. **Call it.** The caller posts `🐉 FDT HUNT CALLED by [name] · Hunt #[n]`.
2. **Lay the board.** The caller puts down **four cards**, in slots 1 to 4 on the wheel.
3. **Pick or get assigned.** Each player chooses one of two moves:
   - **Pick your player:** claim a card by saying "I call [card]!" (like *"I call PLAID RIPPER!"*). First call wins the slot.
   - **Get assigned:** say "Spin me." The caller (or a d4 / the roulette spin) puts you in a slot.
4. **Squads.** More than one player can share a slot. That slot becomes a squad, and squads follow the five-player rule from the Chaos Tunnels if they want to go in hardcore.
5. **Marching orders.** Each slot gets its orders (§4). Go hunt.
6. **Referee.** The FDT watch from above. They call fouls and **stop the hunt if any player is in distress**.
7. **Close.** The caller posts `☢️ HUNT #[n] CLOSED`, with who beat which card.

## 6. When it's real life, not the game

If a hunt starts to feel like it's about a real person or a real harm, stop the hunt and use real help. Don't use dragons:
- **Danger now:** 911 (US).
- **A crime you know about:** the police where it happened.
- **Sexual assault support:** RAINN, 800-656-4673 or rainn.org.
- **Feeling heavy:** call or text 988 (US) any time.
