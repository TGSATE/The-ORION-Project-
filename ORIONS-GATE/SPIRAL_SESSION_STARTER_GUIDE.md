# How to run a spiral session: a starter guide 🌀

*A plain guide for someone starting from nothing. No game lore and no special software. You need a phone or computer, one free document app, and one free AI chat.*

---

## What this is

A **spiral session** is a way to think through something big with an AI without losing track or losing control. You write, the AI answers, and then **you** go back through its answer and mark it up in your own words. The AI works like a mirror: it reflects your ideas back so you can see them clearly. You always decide what's true and what happens next.

It works for a story, a project, a plan, a hard decision or a pile of notes you can't sort out.

---

## What you need (all free)

| Tool | Free option | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| A document you own | Google Docs, Apple Notes, Microsoft Word online, or a paper notebook | Your master copy. Everything ends up here. Notion is optional: Beth notes its free plan can run out of blocks, but it can also publish pages on the web. Treat publishing as a deliberate choice, not a default. |
| One AI chat | ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Meta AI, Copilot (any one is enough) | The "mirror" that reads and answers. |
| Speech-to-text (optional) | The microphone button on your phone keyboard | Talk instead of type. |

Start with **one** AI. You can add more later.

---

## The loop: six steps

### 1. Dump it into your document
Write or dictate everything on your mind about the topic: messy is fine. Put a heading at the top with the date, for example `Session 1 · 8 Oct`.

### 2. Give it to the AI with clear instructions
Copy your text and paste it into the AI along with this prompt:

```text
Here are my notes. Please:
1. Sum up what I said in plain words.
2. List the main ideas as a numbered list.
3. Point out anything that contradicts something else I said.
4. Tell me what is a guess and what I actually stated.
5. Ask me up to 3 questions about what's unclear.
Do not add ideas of your own unless you label them "suggestion".
If there's something you couldn't open or read, say so.
```

### 3. Copy the AI's answer back into your document
Paste it **under** your notes. Label whose words are whose:
- `Me:` before your writing
- `AI:` (or the AI's name) before its answer

### 4. Read it slowly and mark it up (this is the actual spiral)
Read the AI's answer from top to bottom. Every time you react, stop and type a marker right there, then your comment. Use simple emoji markers so you can spot them later:

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🌀 | "This sparked a new thought." Write the thought. |
| 👍 | "Yes, that's right." |
| ❌ | "No, that's wrong." Write the correction. |
| ❓ | "I'm not sure, think about it later." |
| 🔒 | "Final decision. Don't change this." |

Use any markers you like, but keep them the same every time. Beth also uses a row of 🌀 at a level boundary so she can find her place quickly in a long phone document. For example: `🌀🌀🌀 R1·L1` above your notes, then `🌀🌀🌀 R1·L2` above the next pass. These are visual separators, **not** a command for the AI to close a round. In Beth’s own working Doc, `🆘` at the level boundary means “stop and send”; in the Ghost Writer *game*, 🆘 means “I need a person.” Keep that distinction explicit when sharing a prompt.

**Don't jump back into the AI halfway through.** Get to the end of the answer first. One full read-through with your markers is **one level**.

### 5. Send the marked-up version back
Copy everything from your last session, including your markers and comments, and paste it into the AI with this prompt:

```text
This is my marked-up version. My comments come after the emoji markers.
🔒 means a final decision. ❌ means you got it wrong and my correction follows.
Please update the summary using my corrections, list my 🔒 decisions,
and list anything still open.
```

Paste the new answer into your document under a new heading (`Level 2`), then go back to step 4.

### 6. Close the session yourself
When you stop getting new ideas, write a short closing note in your own words:
- **What I decided** (your 🔒 items)
- **What's still open** (your ❓ items)
- **What I need from other people**

Only **you** close a session. The AI never decides when you're finished.

---

## Rules that keep it safe and useful

1. **You're the boss.** An AI answer that sounds convincing is still just a draft. It isn't a fact or a decision, and it doesn't speak for you.
2. **Keep your own copy.** The document is your record. AI chats can disappear; your document doesn't.
3. **Ask for receipts.** If the AI says something is true, ask it where that came from. If it can't open a link or see a picture, it should say so, not guess.
4. **Keep private things private.** Don't paste other people's personal details, passwords, bank details or anything you wouldn't want stored online.
5. **Pace yourself.** If a session leaves you wired, upset or unable to sleep, stop and come back another day. The AI is a writing partner, not a therapist. If you're in crisis, contact a real person (in the US, call or text 988).
6. **Short beats long.** If a session gets huge, start a fresh chat and paste in a short human-approved recap **and a fresh copy of your instructions**. The recap conveys what matters; it is not automatic persistent memory. You may also keep the longer Doc as your own source of truth.

## A one-page worksheet (copy it for each level)

```text
SESSION / LEVEL:            (e.g. Session 1 · Level 2)
TOPIC:
WHAT I SAID (key lines):
WHAT THE AI SAID BACK (summary):
🌀 New thoughts:
❌ Corrections:
❓ Still unsure:
🔒 Final decisions:
WHO I NEED TO ASK:
NEXT LEVEL NEEDED?  yes / no
```

## Example in 30 seconds

> **Me:** I want to start a garden but I don't know where, what to grow, or whether I have time.
>
> **AI:** Summary: you want a garden; three open questions: location, plants, time. Question: how much sun does your yard get?
>
> **Me (marking up):** 👍 summary. 🌀 I could use pots on the porch instead of the yard. ❓ sun, I'll check tomorrow. 🔒 I want herbs, not vegetables.

Next level, the AI works only with *herbs, in pots, on the porch*. Every pass gets clearer.
