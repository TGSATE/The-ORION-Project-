If you're on her team: send one short message at a time, in plain words, and don't ask her to fix or understand code.

## What the confusing phrases mean

- **"No product build" / "nothing built yet"**: none of the coders has turned these ideas into a working app yet. Nothing is broken and nothing was lost. It just means that step hasn't happened.
- **"No proofs" / "no new Lean work"**: sometimes I use a maths-checking tool to confirm that the *rules of the game* are consistent. "No proofs" just means I didn't use it that time. It is not a judgement on the work.
- **"Checked in Lean" / "builds clean"**: the game's rules, as written, were checked and they hold together. It is not a working app, and Beth doesn't need to do anything with it.
