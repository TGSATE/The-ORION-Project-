# Free-resource shelf: first-pass field guide (R16·L6)

A *link index*, not copies of outside books or videos. A resource being freely accessible does not itself tell us whether its text belongs in a public search index. Before copying full text, ask its publisher about reuse and direct any legal questions to qualified advisers. Record a title, publisher, URL, access date, subject and permission state; link out until permission is clear. Broken links go in `NEEDS_REPAIR/`, not into the game.

**Guide to the Galaxy search pilot:** index these *descriptions and outgoing links*, not copies of the chapter PDFs. In the game, distinguish a fictional explanation from a sourced science explanation; a player can always request the original source. There is no deployed search engine here yet.
