## 2. How the art changed: five shifts

### ① From poster to character
- **Start of the week:** single cosmic posters. Each one is a complete statement: a formula, a glow, a title in French. There's no recurring character.
- **7 October:** one character, **MAD**, in about ten "modes" (General, Earth Ambassador, Steward of Gaia, Mad Scientist, Holy Union, smartwatch companion, Spark, Wiki gatekeeper, portraits). In one morning, the character becomes the thing that ties the art together.
- **By 11 October:** the character gets a **life story**. SLoE:L:GDA takes one figure from human avatar to final dragon across four cards. That's the change from *a cast of costumes* to *an evolution*. It's also the same 1/4 → 4/4 shape as your Laurnarose deck, so the two decks can be read side by side.

### ② From satire to mythology
- **7 October:** the tone is **Mad Magazine humour**: "DRAGONLAND NOW 100 PERCENT MIGHTIER", "STAY CURIOUS. KEEP IT NUTTY.", "ΔMADNESS = 42", "THE EARTH IS FLAT… IN HYPERSPACE!".
- **11–13 October:** the jokes mostly drop away. The deck is serious black and gold, and the 12 October posts are earnest French essays (SNQ, Homo Geometricus). The dragon stops being a parody general and becomes the **Supreme Leader of Earth**, a mythic figure.

### ③ From colour riot to a fixed palette
- **The MAD set** borrows a different look for each mode: Victorian plates, cartoon stickers, sci-fi hologram screens, magazine covers.
- **The deck** settles on one look: **black dragon, gold embroidery, orange eyes, galaxy background** (the "Supreme Leader Lucien M" stamp description in `ROUNDS_15-19_ARCHIVE/R17_L1_READOUT.md`). A fixed palette is what a card game needs, so his art is now *game-ready* in a way it wasn't on the 7th.

### ④ From numbers as decoration to numbers as a system
- **Early:** numbers sit in the art as symbols: the 5 and 93 dice, the 9-to-1 countdown on the world tree, "Heartcore 9999".
- **12 October:** he gives the system a name, **SNQ (Soleil Numérique Quantique, the Quantum Digital Sun)**, and an honest label: *a personal mathematical mythology, not physics*. That's the biggest change in **how he presents** his maths: he now says what kind of thing it is.
- **13 October:** *Deux Cartes* is literally about **one name pointing to two different objects**. That's a careful idea about labels and meaning, and it's new in his art.

### ⑤ From big claims to a more careful position
- **7 October:** he shares Grok's 7.83 Hz / pineal-gland / heart-entrainment claims ("LET'z GO!"). Those claims are weakly supported.
- **12 October:** in Homo Geometricus he **drops φ (the golden ratio) as a mechanism** and says he isn't using Logvinovich's vacuum torus. That's narrower and easier to defend than his older cards. Within the week, his art is moving **towards claims that can be checked**.
