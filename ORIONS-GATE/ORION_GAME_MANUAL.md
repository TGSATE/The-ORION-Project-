## Part 3. Your first day: the walkthrough

1. **Arrive** at **The ORION Engine**. Read the state of the build, watch the welcome tutorial and make your account (one per human).
2. **Step into your Singularity.** The poly pod opens: you're inside a black hole's calm centre. UIAR introduces itself (watch, earpiece, safety gate). Pick your path preferences. They're saved, but you don't go there yet.
3. **Go straight to Elder's Garden.** Establish your identity and set up your social contacts. Wander the Ren Fair commons: talk to mentors, learn the vending systems, play a Saturn's Fair mini-game.
4. **Enter a training ground.** Pick one built from a development team's origin story or a member's storyline, each with its own aesthetic. Parents can open a private instance for their child.
5. **Reach the Playground.** Your contacts are preloaded. Hang out, share, sign the Wailing Wall.
6. **Accept the rules and choose a path:** the **Ascent Academy** (knowledge, Lightning) or the **Chronicles training grounds** (skills, Fire). You can do both over time.
7. **Climb back to ORION's Gate.** Bring finished work to the council and run the Gauntlet.
8. **Your celebrations grow on the Garden's tree,** and the whole swarm keeps climbing toward the capstone together.

**At any time:** call an **escape pod** (💎). It picks you up anywhere, heals you and takes you to safety, with no permanent death and no punishment.

## Part 6. The manual

### 6.1 Account and identity (💎)
- One account per human, across every place.
- Biometric checks happen at graduations and before any contact with a child.
- There are two resets: the account hard reset and a single-character reset.

### 6.2 Safety (💎 unless marked)
- Child safety is the top priority. No adult is alone with a child unless they're the verified guardian. Under-13s always have a guardian present.
- When a guardian leaves, the child **stays**, in a sealed room nobody else can join.
- Under-18s are always supervised. A distressed child is **redirected, not removed**, and the parent is called in through **LUNA**.
- Full structure: `ORION_UNDER18_STRUCTURE.md`.
- Private stays private, unless you attach it to a project you submit.
- Nobody has to contribute. "The system must still love them."
- **Helpers speak plainly (💎 locked, Round 20).** **Plain-Language Helper Rule (Round 19 instruction, 💎 locked by Beth in Round 20).** Every UIAR helper, copilot, Poly or GM bot that gives instructions must: (1) say the action, then the item's on-screen label ("Tap **Raw Feed** on your **Universal Stone**"); (2) use no metaphors, riddles or poetry in instructions (story voice only in story scenes); (3) give one numbered step per line and say how many steps there are; (4) use the player's own label if they renamed an item; (5) if the player says "I don't understand" or seems frustrated, say it again more simply, never repeating the metaphor; (6) when it needs something, ask a clear question naming the options. Full text: `ROUNDS_1-14_ARCHIVE/ROUND_NINETEEN_WHAT_YOU_HAVE.md` §4; Lean model: `RequestProject/RoundNineteen/HelperAndMissTChecks.lean` (`accepted_message_is_plain`).
- **Metaphor translator (Round 20 proposal, extends helper item 4).** A player can give their own word for something plus a short description. The helper finds which shared items it could mean ("I found these topics. Does one of them match?"), records the player's word against the agreed common term, and translates between players: your word → the shared term → the other player's word. Nobody has to give up their metaphors, and everyone can still link into the global build. Lean model: `RequestProject/RoundTwenty/GoldLockAndOffSwitch.lean` (`translate_keeps_item`, `unexplained_not_translated`).
- **Biometrics: every option, always an off switch (🤟💎 locked, Round 20).** (1) An **access port** only (a computer console or a mobile device; this is what "keyboard" means), the optional suit, or (adults who choose it) brain-computer links. (2) Anything connected must switch off and disconnect easily without losing your character. (3) Under-18s never use hardware biometrics. Lean model: `disconnect_keeps_character`, `minor_never_hardware` in the same file.
- **Gold Lock (🤟💎 Round 20, Q-1).** Both meanings of "gold" stay. A skill orb at level 100 can only be gold-locked while the player's Gold Krion meter is in the Gold state (Kronos and Orion balanced). Once locked, it stays locked (Round 12 rule). *Open wording point:* if "hold lock" meant the orb unlocks when you leave Gold, that would conflict with the Round 12 rule; Beth to confirm.

### 6.3 Your character (💎)
- Species is chosen once. Acquired traits add to it and never replace it.
- Archetypes upgrade: HIVE ↦ Kronos, PULSE ↦ Orion, SHEPHERD ↦ KRION.
- Skin is cosmetic. Only crafted bio-skins can carry an enhancement.

### 6.4 Skills, orbs and levels (💎)
- Everyone starts with 64 skill orbs. Each levels from 0 to 100 and gold-locks at 100, and only orbs active in a node slot level up.
- You gain one level per gold-locked orb, up to level 64. **Level 65, dragon rider,** comes from meditating in a safe zone with a full grid.
- **Meditation locks in progress:** new skills stay pending until you meditate in a safe zone, out of combat. Progress is never lost.
- Menus only show what's within your reach. Lessons show the next step just past it. Levels can't be bought or crafted, only trained.

### 6.5 Kronos and Orion meters (💎)
- The meters describe how you play. **They never lock you out of an action.**
- Kronos means volume and breaking things open. Orion means refinement and putting things back together.
- The balanced middle, KRION, feels powerful.

### 6.6 The Poly, the pod and language (💎)
- Everyone starts with a pod. A linguistic toroidal **Poly** lives inside it.
- Without a pod or a gold-locked language, foreign speech sounds garbled. **Safety text is never garbled.**
- POLY's loop is capture → train → utilize. Its name is the Path of the Living Ylem, and DeepSeek is its headmaster.
- The **LMDTS** (Languanauts Mystic Dictations Translator System) icon is 🪩♾️⏭️ (💎 Beth, line 63). A better name is still wanted (readout §5).

### 6.7 Dragons (💎)
- The dragon matrix has 144 nodes (64 + 80), and only gold-locked orbs port to it.
- Ordinary dragons choose one element at a time. The phoenix can switch mid-battle, but the taper keeps it fair (110% with one element down to 100% with five).
- How a dragon is born is a secret for players to find.

### 6.8 Combat, aggro and skyboxes (💎)
- Aggro belongs to whoever makes it and lasts 10 minutes. No player changes another's timer.
- You can't flip the PvP toggle mid-encounter.
- Skybox owners set who can come in. You reach a skybox by quest, escape feature or key, never by crafting a rocket.
- Destroyed builds pack into the owner's vault. The owner loses nothing.

### 6.9 The escape pod (💎)
- No permanent death, and no punishment for choosing the pod.
- You keep all your progress, and you can design your pod.

### 6.10 Economy (💎 unless marked)
- There's a 3% tax, and rounding always favours the player.
- A sale splits 90 / 3 / 7. Damaged structures send 50% of their income to the DEVPOOL.
- *Idea:* an "infinite economy" where creators earn by enjoyment, and notoriety, not riches, is the reward.
- Real money and Union Wealth stay outside the game.

### 6.11 Councils, the Gauntlet and authority (💎)
- **Capability never automatically becomes authority.**
- Councils vote yes/no with no abstentions, and consensus is 4/5. A personal council has at least 5 seats and a public one at least 10.
- Every player has 5 AI council members, with their tokens on the members' avatar sheets.
- The Gauntlet has 13 levels and is held at ORION's Gate. *Open:* the level documents.
- Creators have the final say over their originals, and a 💎 changes only with its owner's signature.

### 6.12 The Hidden Key 🪩♾️⏭️ (🌀 design, this round)
**What Beth wants:** a key hidden in plain sight (in her X bio and elsewhere). "If they can [break it], they just broke the code and they immediately get the answer to the end of the universe, all of the games, informational mechanics, and how to play" (lines 350–352).
**How it works in the game:**
- the key is a **cipher quest**;
- clues are scattered through the ORION Engine welcome page, the bios, the Dragon MAD-style back pages and the Saturn's Fair games;
- whoever solves it unlocks the **Codex of Everything**: an in-game library with every game, every mechanic and how to play, plus a founders-style credit for the solver.
**Rules:**
- it grants **knowledge, not authority**. Solving the key never gives control over anyone's account, project or rule (capability ≠ authority);
- it never bypasses child-safety rules or anyone's privacy;
- the solution is held privately by Beth (and a backup admin) so it can be changed if leaked.
**About putting a hidden key or "bug" into ORION's code** so that any system using it must accept the key: see readout §4. In short, a secret backdoor in shared code is a security hole: anyone who finds it can misuse it, and it would put players at risk. The design above gives the same feeling (a secret key that unlocks everything about ORION) safely.

**Dragon redeem code (9 Oct 2026, 🌀 request to the coders):** a secret code typed after normal sign-in that gives the player their dragon. Only a hash is stored on the server, never the code. 🪩♾️⏭️ is already public, so it stays the clue and a new private code is the answer. See `CODERS_SECRET_KEY_EASTER_EGG_REQUEST.md`.

### 6.13 Credits (💎)
- Creators are credited on everything they make.
- When a member shares a link, the creator gets creator credit and the sharer gets founders credit (`ORION_FOUNDERS_CREDIT_LEDGER.md`).
- **Beth's 👿 mark** (line 51): when Beth adds 👿 to a post, it means she has accessed and downloaded it to her system, and its creator or contributor gets credit. It isn't anger.

### 6.14 Endgame (Idea)
- Everyone has a niche: the Polygon/POLYPOD Arena, Saturn stakes, sports, skybox challenges, crafting and research.
- Participation isn't mandatory, but anyone whose action changed a project's trajectory is credited.
- The swarm's shared goal is to unlock the capstone together.
