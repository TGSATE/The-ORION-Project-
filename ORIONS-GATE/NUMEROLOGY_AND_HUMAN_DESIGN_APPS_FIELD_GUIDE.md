# Field Guide: two self-discovery apps 🔢🧬

*Written at OR19.2 (9 October 2026), from the two App Store links in the "Loggie's report" section of your Doc. You asked for: "Field guide on how to utilize these apps what they are capable of what their payment plans and free tiers look like."*

**What I read:** each app's US App Store listing (description, ratings and in-app purchase list), on 9 October 2026. **I haven't used either app.** Prices are what the US store shows today. They change, and other countries differ.

**The honest frame:** numerology and Human Design are reflection tools, not science. They're good for prompts, game flavour and conversation starters, and they can't predict anything. That fits how ORION already uses numerology (`BETH_NUMEROLOGY_LOG_FOR_TEAM.md`): as a creative layer.

---

## 1. Numerology: Rediscover Yourself (by Mirofox)

https://apps.apple.com/app/id1584354336

**What it does:** works out the classic numerology numbers from your birth date and full name, then explains them:
- **Life Path** (from your birth date);
- **Expression / Destiny** (from your full name);
- **Soul Urge / Heart's Desire**;
- **Attitude number**;
- **Day of birth** number;
- plus further readings described in the listing.

**Rating:** 4.8 out of 5 (about 2,300 ratings), age 4+, in the Lifestyle category.

**Free vs paid:**
- **Free:** the download and the basic numbers.
- **Paid "Numerology Pro"** unlocks the rest. The store lists several Pro options: $3.59, $4.49, $12.99, $19.99 and $26.99. These are most likely different subscription lengths plus a one-time unlock, but the listing doesn't say which is which, so check inside the app before you buy.
- **Tips:** "Coffee" $2.99 and "Lunch" $8.99. These are just donations to the developer.
- **Family Sharing:** some purchases can be shared.

**How to use it for ORION:**
1. Enter a character's name and "birthday" to give them a Life Path number for their card (`ORION_PLAYER_CHARACTER_SHEETS.md`).
2. Use the free numbers as **spiral prompts**: read your Soul Urge description, then spiral on what fits and what doesn't.
3. Log anything that sparks in the numerology log, as you already do.

**Watch out for:** subscriptions renew automatically. Cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

## 2. Human Design: Stella (by Viyatek)

https://apps.apple.com/app/id1671519783

**What it does:** draws your **Human Design chart** (the "bodygraph") from your birth date, time and place. It shows:
- your type, strategy and authority;
- your energy centres, channels and gates;
- **daily affirmations** and guidance;
- **compatibility readings** with partners, friends and children;
- **"Stella AI"**, a built-in chatbot you can ask questions about your chart.

**Rating:** 4.8 out of 5 (about 2,600 ratings), age 4+, in the Lifestyle category.

**Free vs paid:**
- **Free:** the download and a basic chart.
- **Most of the depth and Stella AI sit behind a subscription.** The store lists:
  - Weekly: $4.99 (some offers at $7.99);
  - Monthly: $14.99;
  - Yearly: $29.99, $39.99 or $49.99, depending on the offer.
- **Tip:** weekly plans add up to about $260 a year at $4.99. If you want it, a yearly plan is far cheaper. Watch for free trials that turn into paid weekly plans.

**How to use it for ORION:**
1. Use **compatibility readings** as flavour for squad pairings (lore only, never a rule that decides who can play with whom).
2. Ask Stella AI the same question you ask your own council and compare the answers. It's a nice Ghost Rider exercise in "disagreement is information".
3. The bodygraph's centres-and-channels look is a good visual reference for the Mystic Numerology art style (`GHOST_RIDER_TUTORIAL_MEDIA_PACK.md` A3).

**Watch out for:** it needs your exact birth time and place. That's personal data, so read the app's privacy policy, and don't enter other people's birth details without asking them.

## Side by side

| | Numerology (Mirofox) | Human Design: Stella (Viyatek) |
|---|---|---|
| Needs | Name and birth date | Birth date, **time** and place |
| Free | Core numbers | Basic chart |
| Paid | "Pro": $3.59 to $26.99 options | Subscription: $4.99/week, $14.99/month, $29.99 to $49.99/year |
| AI inside | No | Yes ("Stella AI") |
| Best ORION use | Character numbers, spiral prompts | Squad flavour, art reference, council comparison |
| Rating | 4.8 (about 2.3k) | 4.8 (about 2.6k) |

**Cheapest way to try both:** use the free parts for a week. Only buy if you keep opening them, and pick a one-time or yearly option over weekly.
