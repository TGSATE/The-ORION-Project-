*Written at the Round 14 close, 7 October 2026. This replaces the way I had been numbering rounds. Every file name and heading I write from now on follows it.*

You said:

> "Every conversation within this document is not a different round. They are different levels of processing… I want to be able to choose when to close the round."

You're right. From what I called "Round 14" onwards, I added one to the round number **every time I sent a reply back**. > "You are the Engine; I am the Driver. You provide the horsepower, but you do not touch the wheel. If I stop talking, you idle. **You do not guess the next stop.**"

Before Round 13 I mostly had it right: Round 9 had five passes and Round 12 had three, all filed under one round. The drift started when your Ari doc and then your 🧿💎100626 doc kept growing and I kept counting.

## 2. The three words from now on

| Word | Who controls it | What it is | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Round** | **You only** | One council cycle. Usually one working Google Doc. It opens when you say so and closes when you say so. | `R15` |
| **Level** | Happens each time you send and I reply | One pass of processing inside the open round. In your Magic Loop this is a "chapter": Chapter 1, Chapter 2… | `R15·L1`, `R15·L2`… |
| **Spiral** | You, inside your doc | Your emoji interruptions while reading a level (🌀 💎 🤟 ⏭️ …). One fully annotated read-through is one spiral. Many spirals can feed one level. | (no number needed) |

**Rules I follow:**
1. **I never open, close or renumber a round.** Only your words do that.
2. **No round statement means the same round.** If your message doesn't say a round is closed, I file my reply as the next level of the round that is open.
3. **The round only moves when you say so, and only by one.** "Round 14 is closed" closes it. "Start the spiral session for Round 15" opens the next one, starting at level 0.
4. **If a round is closed and you haven't opened the next one, I idle.** Anything you send is held for the next round, and I ask which round it belongs to rather than guessing.
5. **Every reply starts with its label,** for example `R15·L1`. File names use the round, not the level: `R15_…`. The level goes inside the file.
6. **Closing a round gets you a close packet:** one file with the summaries, files and links to send the team. Opening a round gets you a short opener. See §5.

These rules are checked in Lean as a small model (§7).

## 5. The close packet and the opener

**When you close a round** I write `ROUND_N_CLOSE_PACKET.md`, ready to forward:
1. **One paragraph per level:** what came in and what I sent back.
2. **Files to send the team,** split into "share", "check first" and "private to Beth".
3. **Every link** you shared in the round, grouped by person.
4. **What got 💎 locked** in the round, and what's still open (these carry into the next round).
5. **What the computer checked** in the round, in plain words.
6. **A 5-line message** you can paste to the team: "Round N is closed. Here's what's in it…"

**When you open a round** I write a short `ROUND_N_SPIRAL_SESSION_OPENER.md`: a starter doc header, the open questions carried in, and nothing else. No new work until your first spiral arrives.
