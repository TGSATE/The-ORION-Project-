# ORION founders-credit ledger

**The rule (Beth, Round 12):** "If one of the team members shares a link, not only does that creator get credit, the team member who shared the link gets a founders credit as well."

**How this ledger works**
- **Creator credit** goes to whoever made the linked work, as the work itself names them. That isn't always the account that posted it.
- **Founders credit** goes to the person who brought the link into ORION.
- A founders credit is a **recognition record**. It isn't a share, a payment, a vote or authority over anything. "Notoriety instead of riches" (Round 12).
- A person who shares their own work gets creator credit only. Counting it twice would double-credit them.
- **Open question for Beth (Q-R12-3):** the rule says "team member". Does a founders credit also go to **contributors** who share (for example Shakes, Hopegonedark), or only to the development team (Mike, Matthew, Lucien, Ryan) and Beth? Until you answer, contributor rows are marked **provisional**.
