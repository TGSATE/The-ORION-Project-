## The idea

Every member picks **two short emoji codes**:

| Call | Means | Beth's (proposed reading) |

| **Play call** | "Come and play / chat / spiral with me, no pressure" | **👻🌪️🏍️** (the terrified, tiny tornado ghost rider) |

Written together, the **|** separates them: `👻🌪️🏍️|🤟💎`. **O-5: Beth, is that the right way round?** I've guessed play = ghost rider and build = diamond, because 💎 is already your "lock it" mark.

## How it would work

**Today (no game yet), between people:**
- Put your two codes on your character sheet (`ORION_PLAYER_CHARACTER_SHEETS.md` now has a line for them) and in your X bio if you like.
- In a group chat or doc, start a message with someone's code to get their attention in the right mode: `🤟💎 Beth, the Greedy rules need your check`.

**In ORION (later, a design proposal):**
- Each player's codes live on their profile.
- A **play call** shows as a soft notification the player can ignore.
- A **build call** goes to a "build inbox", and if it's unanswered for a while, a reminder.
- The player sets quiet hours, and can turn each call type off.

## Safety rules (important)

1. **An emoji code is not a password.** Anyone who has seen it can type it. It only means "please look", never "let me in". It can never unlock an account, approve anything, or prove who someone is.
2. **No calls between unconnected adults and children.** For under-18 players, only their verified guardian and approved mentors in approved spaces can call them (`ORION_UNDER18_STRUCTURE.md`).
3. **Limits against spam:** a few calls per person per hour, and the Playground spam filter applies.
4. **Block and mute** always work, whatever the code.
5. **Nobody is obliged to answer.** Not responding is allowed. The "if I'm not responding" idea means *friends know how to reach me*, not *the system keeps pushing*.
