# T04. Running a round: the annotate-and-return loop 🌀🪞

*Round 16, 7 October 2026. The full version, with a worksheet, is `GHOST_WRITER_IN_THE_MIRROR.md`. This is the quick card.*

## The loop

1. **Start a Google Doc** (anyone with the link can view). Put a title line such as `🧿🧿🧿💎100626` and `Ari`.
2. **Paste in the last summary** you got back.
3. **Interrupt it with your thoughts**, right where they come up, marked with your emoji:
   - 💎 lock this · 🤟 / 👍 yes, agreed · 🌀 new idea / spiral · 🍝 spaghetti / not sure what this means · 🤷‍♀️ don't know / your call · ☢️ urgent or worrying · ✋ stop, that's wrong.
4. **Add links** (posts, playlists, repos, docs) with one line saying what each is and what you want done.
5. **Add new jobs** at the end, in plain words.
6. **Share the doc link** in the request.
7. **Read the readout** that comes back. It starts with a table showing where each of your notes went.
8. **Repeat until *you* decide it's done.** Each repeat is a **level** of the same round (R15·L1, R15·L2…), not a new round.
9. **Close it in words:** "Round 15 is closed." You get a close packet to send the team. Then "Start the spiral session for Round 16" opens the next one.

> **Round 14 close update:** rounds are yours. I never open, close or renumber a round; each reply is a level inside the round you have open. Full rules, commands and the relabelling of old rounds: `ROUND_SYSTEM.md`. Running the council with several AIs: `GHOST_RIDER_PROTOCOL_PROOFS_MODEL_EDITION.md` §4.

## Tips that save time

- **One idea per paragraph**, so each one can be traced.
- **Say who a link is from** ("Lu", "Matthew", "Bobby").
- **Use the decision IDs** (C-03, O-2…) when you answer a question: "O-2 💎 yes".
- **Dictation slips are fine.** Recurring ones are in the glossary: "crayon" = **KRION**, "Dove team" = **DEV team**, "tomato" = Meta ("meadow": Meta or Alexa, still to confirm, L-11), "Ryne" = Ryan, "Solutions" = Lucien.
- **Private things:** if you don't want something written down, say so in the note and it won't be (as with the FDT letters).
- **Images:** prompts you paste in are recorded as text. The images themselves are read from the doc or the shared album.
