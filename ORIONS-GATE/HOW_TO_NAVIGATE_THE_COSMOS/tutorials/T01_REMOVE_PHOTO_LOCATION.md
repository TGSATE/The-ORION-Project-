# T01. Taking hidden location data off photos 📍🚫

*Round 16, 7 October 2026. Menu names change between phone and app versions; if a step doesn't match your screen, look for the nearest wording.*

## What it is, in one paragraph

When a phone camera takes a photo, it can save extra information **inside the picture file**: the date, the phone model, and often **the exact place it was taken** (GPS coordinates). You can't see it in the picture itself, but anyone who downloads the **original file** can read it with free tools. That can reveal where you live or work. It's called **metadata** (or EXIF data).

## Option A. Delete the photos from the shared album (simplest)

**Google Photos (phone):**
1. Open the shared album.
2. Tap and hold the first photo to select it, then tap the others.
3. Tap the **⋮** or **trash/remove** button and choose **Remove from album**. (This removes them from the album for everyone. Your own copy stays in your library unless you delete it too.)

## Option B. Share a screenshot instead of the original

A screenshot is a brand-new picture, and on iPhone it **doesn't carry the original photo's location**.
1. Open the photo full screen.
2. Take a screenshot (iPhone with Face ID: press the **side button and volume up** together).
3. Crop the screenshot if you want, and add **that** to the album instead of the original.

## Option C. Remove the location from the original

**On iPhone (Photos app):**
1. Open the photo.
2. Swipe up, or tap the **ⓘ** button. A small map shows where it was taken.
3. Tap **Adjust** next to the location, then choose **No Location**.
4. For several photos at once: select them, tap **•••**, choose **Adjust Location**, then **No Location**.

**When sending from iPhone:** tap **Share**, then **Options** at the top of the share screen, and switch **Location** off before sending.

**In Google Photos:** open the photo, swipe up to see its details, and look for the location line with an edit (pencil) option such as **Remove location**. Google Photos settings also have a privacy option about hiding location data in items you share; its exact name and place vary, and it may only affect new shares. Deleting or replacing the original is the surest route.

## Stop it happening again (optional)

**iPhone:** Settings → **Privacy & Security** → **Location Services** → **Camera** → **Never**. New photos won't record where they were taken. (You lose the "photos on a map" feature, which some people like. Your choice.)
