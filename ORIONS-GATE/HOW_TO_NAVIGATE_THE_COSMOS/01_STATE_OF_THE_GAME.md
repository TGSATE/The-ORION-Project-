## The map (locked names)

1. **The ORION Engine**: the welcome page and the umbrella system. You land here first. 💎
2. **The Singularity**: your private home hub.
3. **Elder's Garden**: the ancestral tree and celebrations, and the central hub.
4. **The Playground**: the social and creative community. No ads, no spam. 💎
5. **The ORION Chronicles**: the MMO.
6. **The Ascent**: education and skills.
7. **ORION's Gate**: community projects and the Gauntlet.
8. **UIAR**: Universally Intelligent Augmented Reality. 💎

Proposed (not 💎) launch path: Engine → Singularity → Elder's Garden → Playground → Ascent or Chronicles → ORION's Gate (F-4 / M-3).
