# 02. ORION in one page 🌌

*For someone who has never heard of it. Last checked against the manual: Round 16, 7 October 2026.*

**ORION (the ORION Engine Global Project)** is a planned online world that joins a game, a school, a social space and a community-projects board into one place, under one account per human.

**The idea underneath it:** people of every age learn, create and play together, with AI as a helper ("humans as the driver and AI as the speed and strength"), and **capability never automatically becomes authority**. Getting good at something lets you propose and contribute; it never lets you quietly take control of anyone else's life, character or project.

## The eight places

| Place | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **The ORION Engine** | Arrive. Read the welcome, the tutorials, the credits and the state of the build. Make your account. |
| **The Singularity** | Your private home: arrange your heads-up display, see your characters, choose your paths. |
| **Elder's Garden** | The central hub and the ancestral tree. Set up your identity, meet mentors at the fair, train, and celebrate finished work as glowing "life-bubbles". |
| **The Playground** | Hang out, share art and theories, start ideas. No ads, no spam. |
| **The ORION Chronicles** | The MMO: skills, orbs, dragons, skyboxes, crafting and the dragon wars. |
| **The Ascent** | Learn at your own pace, switch subjects any time. |
| **ORION's Gate** | Bring finished projects to the Galactic Council. A project goes into the real world only with the council's agreement *and* the humans' yes. |
| **UIAR** | The augmented-reality layer: map, translation, and immersion that only goes deep when you're somewhere safe. |

## What makes it different

- **Child safety comes first.** No adult is alone with a child unless they're the verified guardian. Under-18 and over-18 spaces are separate unless a parent allows otherwise.
- **The Poly:** a language companion that grows with you, from an organism in a black pod to, one day, a golden dragon egg.
- **Three energies:** **Kronos** (fire, high kinetic), **KRION** (earth, water, air, community) and **Orion** (lightning, whimsical knowledge flow). Each place leans towards one.
- **Councils and the Gauntlet:** groups of humans and AIs review work in the open, and nobody sits on top.
- **Credit for creators:** whoever made something is named on it, permanently.
- **Free to play is the goal**, funded by real products made through the build.

## Where it stands

It's a detailed design with lots of concept art and a team of volunteers. **It isn't playable yet.** The next step is a small playable piece (see `04_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md`).
