## 1. The novel in one paragraph (my summary)

Humanity has survived two invasions by an insect-like alien species, the Formics, which it doesn't understand. To prepare for a third, it trains gifted children at an orbiting **Battle School**. They play zero-gravity war games in the **Battle Room** and a strange computer **"mind game"** that reads each child. Ender Wiggin, a gentle but brilliant boy, is pushed, isolated and manipulated by the adults until he leads his friends (his "jeesh") through what he believes are simulations. The final "exam" was real: he has wiped out the Formics. Then he learns they never meant a third war. After the first contact they had understood that humans were thinking beings, but they couldn't talk to us. Ender finds the last Formic queen's cocoon and spends his life as the **Speaker for the Dead**, telling their true story and looking for a world where she can live again.

## 3. Story beats for the dragons (draft)

1. **The First Contact misunderstanding.** Leviathan (fire and water) mistakes the whales' song for a threat, and the WAR begins.
2. **The Dragon School.** Young dragons (the "Dragon Tales" kids grown up, if that collaboration happens) train at Saturn's Fair under Gemini's storytelling.

6. **The Speaker.** Gaia (Ariel–Eve–Gaia–Milky Way) asks the dragons to tell the whole story at Elder's Garden. Leviathan gets "The Way Back" (Bobby's mechanic).
7. **The Guardians.** The dragons take their posts at ORION's Gate.

## 4. Orson Scott Card collaboration: what to know

- Beth hopes to "loop in Orson Scott Card and run an Ender's Game, *Planet Called Treason* and *Hart's Hope* build". For accuracy: ***A Planet Called Treason*** (1979) was revised as ***Treason*** (1988), and ***Hart's Hope*** (1983) is a standalone fantasy novel. They aren't a trilogy, but they could be a "Card worlds" bundle.
- Using his characters, names or text in ORION would need **permission from the rights holders**, usually through the author's literary agent. Until then, ORION uses the *themes* above in its own words and characters. This is a flag, not legal advice.
- A draft approach letter is in `COLLABORATOR_PRODUCTION_PROPOSALS.md` §4.
- **One more thing to know before reaching out:** the author's public statements on some social issues have drawn strong criticism over the years, and some fans and partners avoid working with him for that reason. That's for Beth and the team to weigh. I'm noting it so it isn't a surprise.
