Privacy, in plain words

Everything you type stays in your own browser on your own device (browser “local storage”). This app has no server, no accounts, no tracking and no ads. Nothing you write is uploaded anywhere by this app.

The 🆘 cool-off. If something you write sounds like you might hurt yourself or someone else, the app pauses the “next” button for a while (5 minutes the first time, longer if it keeps happening). It checks your words on your own device only: nothing is sent to anyone, nobody is alerted, and the crisis line stays on screen the whole time. The cool-off record is kept in the same local storage and is deleted when you clear this site's data.

Your touchstone. If you save a trusted person's name and number in the 🆘 card, they are kept in the same local storage on your device. The app never texts anyone by itself: tapping the text button opens your own messages app with a message ready, and you choose whether to send it.

When you press Copy for my AI chat, your session goes to your clipboard. If you paste it into an AI chat, that AI company's own privacy rules apply to what you paste.

To delete everything: press New session, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Fundraiser, support, shop and booking buttons open other websites (for example GoFundMe, Ko-fi or Gumroad); their own privacy rules apply there.

This is a writing game, not therapy. If you are in crisis in the US, call or text 988.
