## 3. The first 90 days

**Days 1–30: one product, one page.**
- Turn `GHOST_WRITER_LOAD_IN_PACKET.md` into a free one-page PDF with your name and links.
- Make a free Ko-fi page (it takes donations and sells files).
- Post the free PDF to X, TikTok and the blog with one line: "the writing game I use with AI; free".

**Days 31–60: the paid version.**
- Write the workbook: the packet + 10 worksheets + 3 worked examples from your own spirals (only ones you're happy to publish).
- Offer 5 "founding" sessions at a low price in exchange for honest feedback.

**Days 61–90: decide what's working.**
- Count: downloads, sales, sessions booked, messages from strangers.
- Keep whichever offer got the most "yes". Drop the rest for now.

## 5. Protect your energy (this is part of the plan)

- **Cap it:** a maximum number of sessions a week, with days off.
- **Boundaries in writing:** "This is a writing practice, not therapy." Point clients to real help when they need it.
- **Batch the work:** record or write once, sell many times (offers 1 and 3), so income doesn't depend on your hardest days.

## 6. Credit and ownership

- Your method and story are yours. Keep the credit line ("A. Beth Jones, TGSATE") on everything you share.
- Collaborators' work stays credited to them (`ORION_FOUNDERS_CREDIT_LEDGER.md`). Before you sell anything that includes their work, get their OK in writing.
- Questions about trademarks, contracts or business registration are legal questions. Ask a free legal clinic or an attorney (§7).

## 7. Free help that exists for exactly this (US)

- **Vocational Rehabilitation (VR):** every state has a VR agency for people with disabilities. Many can support a **self-employment plan**, including training and start-up help. Search "[your state] vocational rehabilitation self-employment".
- **Benefits counselling (WIPA):** if you ever receive or apply for SSDI or SSI, free Work Incentives Planning and Assistance counsellors explain how earnings affect benefits. Call the Ticket to Work helpline, 1-866-968-7842.
- **SCORE:** free business mentors, online or in person: https://www.score.org
- **Small Business Development Centers (SBDC):** free advising, usually through a local college.
- **211:** call or text 211 for local help with rent, food, utilities and legal clinics.
