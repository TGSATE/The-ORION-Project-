## 1. ORION in one minute

ORION is a shared world for learning, play and building. It has seven places:

| Place | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The ORION Engine** | the welcome hall: tutorials, credits, the manifesto, the state of the build |
| **The Singularity** | each player's private home lobby |
| **The Playground** | the social front porch (no ads; the Recruitment Skybox is attached) |
| **The Orion Chronicles** | the game world: quests, dragons, orbs, combat, the economy |
| **The Ascent** | education at your own speed, with switchable curricula |
| **ORION's Gate** | community projects reviewed by the Galactic Council |
| **Elder's Garden** | the ancestral tree of memories and achievements |

**Planets:** Earth (**KRION**), the soft, social homeworld; Mars (**Kronos**), the cold, kinetic outpost; Saturn (**ORION**), the abstract, high-difficulty ring world.

**Rules that shape every story:**
- **No permanent death.** An **escape pod** always rescues you, and choosing it is never punished. 💎
- **Capability never automatically becomes authority.** 💎 Being powerful doesn't make you the ruler.
- **Under-18s are always supervised.** A child in trouble is *redirected, not removed*. The LUNA daycare monitor links in a parent.
- **Humans are the driver, AI is the speed and strength.**

## 5. Your first lore-building kit

**Three starter prompts**
1. *The Way Back.* Write the quest where a player who chose the dark path is invited home by Gabrielle. What does she say? What must they do? Remember: no punishment, only invitation.
2. *The First Apprentice.* A new player arrives at the ORION Engine. Gabrielle is their first mentor. Write the first ten minutes, using your Act I beats (the call, the refusal, the return to ordinary life).
3. *The Seven Rooms as a dungeon.* Turn bridge, working interval, higher watch, far seeing, gate, octave and last approach into seven quest rooms. Give each room one lesson and one test from your Atlas exam (refusal, stability, return, bound, noise).
