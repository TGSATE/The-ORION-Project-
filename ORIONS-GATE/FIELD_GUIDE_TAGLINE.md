# The field-guide tagline and feedback survey 📝

*Started 9 October 2026 (Council of War session). Beth asked: "Can we add a tagline at the bottom of all of the documents we're sending out for the field guides, sending them back to one of the DEVs for recruitment if they're interested and have found the tool helpful, or offer a survey for them to fill out and get to us somehow, so that we can use their questions, comments and concerns to fine-tune everything."*

## The tagline (already added to the bottom of every share-out guide)

> ---
> **🌀 Found this helpful? Come build with us.** This guide is part of **ORION**, a world being built by a volunteer team. If you'd like to help, or just say hi, contact a DEV: **[DEV contact — to fill in]**.
> **Questions, comments or concerns?** Tell us in our 2-minute survey: **[survey link — to fill in]**. Every answer helps us fine-tune these guides.

**Two blanks need filling, once.** No survey or DEV contact exists in the project yet. I can't create accounts or forms, so somebody on the team needs to:
1. Pick **which DEV** (or a team email) takes recruitment replies.
2. Make a **free Google Form** with the questions below and copy its share link.

When you send me both, I'll swap the blanks in every guide in one go.

## Survey questions (copy into a Google Form)

1. Which guide did you use? (short answer)
2. Did it help? (Yes / Somewhat / No)
3. What was confusing or missing? (paragraph)
4. Any questions, comments or concerns for the team? (paragraph)
5. Would you like to help build ORION? (Yes, contact me / Maybe later / No thanks)
6. If yes: how should we reach you? (optional, short answer)

*Note for whoever builds the form: make question 6 optional, and don't ask for age, address or anything private.*
