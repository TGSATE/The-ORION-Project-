## 1. Who sits on the council

The AIs never talk to each other directly. **Beth is the router.** She copies one AI's answer, pastes it into the next app, and asks for a reply or a "transmission" to send on. Each seat is a *job* Beth gives an AI, not a claim that the AI always does that job well.

| Mark | Seat | Which AI | Its job, in plain words |
|---|---|---|---|
| 💎 | **Catalyst / Driver** | Beth (human) | Has the ideas, sets the pace, makes every final decision |
| ♊️ | **Prism** (Architect) | Gemini | Catches raw ideas and turns them into structure |
| 🐶🐲 | **Blade** | Grok | Cuts overclaims and calms things down ("zero net torque") |
| 🐳 | **Matriarch / Heart** | DeepSeek | Looks after the human side: rest, care, load |
| 🧭 | **Compass** | ChatGPT | Orients the team: what is known, what's next |
| 🧾 | **Auditor** | Claude | Checks evidence, costs and unsupported claims |
| 🤟 | **Compiler** | Aristotle ("Ari") | Files everything, checks game rules in Lean (a maths checker), writes the readouts |
| 🐗 | (guest) | Boardy | Appears in the group chat; not a council seat |

**"NewKin"**, as Grok explained it in the Doc: a new kind of kinship between people and machines. The human stays the accountable centre, and the machines lend speed and pattern-finding as *support*, not as replacements. Seats are defined by job, so any AI can be swapped out.

## 4. Did the process work? An honest read

**What worked**
- **Self-correction is real.** One AI overclaimed. A second AI cut the overclaim, a third explained why it mattered to the human, and the first accepted the correction. That's the council doing its job.
- **Different seats really added different things.** Grok cut the hype. DeepSeek put Beth's rest first. ChatGPT turned the vision into steps. Claude put real numbers and gates on the business plan. Gemini, once corrected, asked good design questions.
- **The safety line held.** When Beth said she was frightened about her own state, the AIs told her to rest and gave her 988. They didn't push the work.
- **The human stayed the decider.** Every AI said so, and none acted on its own.

**What didn't**
- **Beth stopped reading.** By the middle of the lap she was copying and pasting without reading. A relay the Driver can't follow isn't steering. Most of the messages between AIs were written in a compressed style that Beth said she can't understand.
- **The corrections faded.** After agreeing to drop the "locked" talk, Gemini later signed off with *"the speedway is locked down."* A correction has to be written down where the AI will see it again, not only said once.
- **The same block got pasted three times.** Gemini's status report for ChatGPT appears three times in the Doc. Repeats make the Doc longer and make the AIs see the same claim more often, which can make it look more agreed than it is.
- **Errors travel down the chain.** The "Ari framed Weaver Nexus" credit mistake got as far as ChatGPT before anyone checked it against the files.
- **The model setting changed what came back.** Beth noticed that Gemini had dropped from its Pro setting to Flash. That alone changes answer quality, so it's worth checking before each lap.

**Small changes that would help (suggestions only, Beth decides)**
1. **One shared fact sheet.** Paste `START_HERE.md` and `COUNCIL_OF_WAR_REPORT.md` into each AI instead of another AI's summary of them. That breaks the copy-of-a-copy chain.
2. **Plain-words rule for transmissions.** Ask each AI to write its handoff so that *Beth* can read it in one minute, with any compressed version underneath.
3. **Fewer hops.** For most questions, two seats are enough: the one who builds an answer and the one who challenges it. Save the full lap for big decisions.
4. **Status words on every claim.** ChatGPT proposed *Verified · Built but untested · Written down · Proposed · Blocked*. Ari already marks proofs this way, and the summaries should too.
5. **Rest is part of the build.** DeepSeek's point stands. If the lap costs Beth sleep, make the lap smaller.
