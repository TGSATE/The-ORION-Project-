## 5. Resource list: languages, dialects and metaphysics

I've kept this to very well-known, widely available sources. Look for library or free online copies.

### Language and dialects
- **The Oxford English Dictionary**: word histories (where a word came from)
- **Merriam-Webster** (free online): definitions and word origins
- **Online Etymology Dictionary** (etymonline.com, free): quick word origins
- **Ethnologue**: catalogue of the world's languages
- **The International Phonetic Alphabet** (International Phonetic Association): how sounds are written down
- **David Crystal, *The Cambridge Encyclopedia of Language***: a friendly overview of how languages work
- **Wiktionary** (free): words in many languages, with pronunciations

### History of magic and esoteric ideas (the ones she posts about)
- ***Picatrix*** (Ghāyat al-Ḥakīm, "The Aim of the Sage"). She posts about it herself (posts 4–5). English translations exist
- **John Dee, *Monas Hieroglyphica*** (1564). The Monad symbol in her posts 20–21
- **Frances Yates, *Giordano Bruno and the Hermetic Tradition***: a classic history of Renaissance magic
- **The *Corpus Hermeticum*** (Hermes Trismegistus texts; many translations)
- **The Egyptian *Book of the Dead***: Amenti, Thoth and the Wedjat eye in posts 2 and 6. The British Museum's material is a good start

### Numerology, astrology, tarot, crystals
- **Numerology:** the Pythagorean tradition. The **Tetractys** (1+2+3+4 = 10) in her post 22 is its best-known symbol
- **Astrology:** Ptolemy's ***Tetrabiblos*** is the oldest standard textbook. The **Sabian Symbols** (Marc Edmund Jones, later Dane Rudhyar) are what her post 28 uses
- **Tarot:** the **Rider–Waite–Smith deck** and A. E. Waite's ***The Pictorial Key to the Tarot*** (1910, now free online)
- **Crystals:** for the real science of a stone (like the beryl in her post 36), use the **Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History** and **mindat.org**. Crystal *meanings* are tradition, not science, so it's best to label them that way

### Theology
- **The Bible**, with a study edition such as the ***New Oxford Annotated Bible***
- **The *Catechism of the Catholic Church*** (free online at vatican.va). It explains the Immaculate Conception versus the Virgin Birth, which is her post 35
- **Encyclopaedia Britannica** (free articles) for any religion or tradition

### Real science behind her symbols
- **Muons, muonium and μSR** (posts 31, 40): CERN and the Paul Scherrer Institute have public pages
- **The Rocket Equation** (her navigation posters): NASA Glenn Research Center's "Beginner's Guide to Rockets" (free)
