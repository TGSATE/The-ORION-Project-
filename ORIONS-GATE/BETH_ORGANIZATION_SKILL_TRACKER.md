# Field guide: Beth's organization skill, level by level 📈

*Started R17·L1, 9 October 2026. Your ask: "if there's any way you can track my organization skill as it progresses and gets more clean, could you please take note of that and put it in its own document in a field guide somewhere."*

**What this page is:** a running log of how your Docs get easier to process, so you can see your own progress. Each level I add one row. I only note things I can point to in your Doc (a heading, a marker, an order). I don't grade you, and nothing here goes to anyone else unless you share it.

**What I look for (the six signals):**
1. **Markers:** level and stop markers (`17.1`, `🆘`, `⏭️`) that tell me where a level starts and stops.
2. **Labels:** icons on links and lines (💎 locked, 🤟 yes, 🧐 check, 🌀 add, ☢️ urgent, ❌ change).
3. **Grouping:** related items under one heading (by person, group or topic).
4. **One ask per line:** each request is its own line or paragraph, so none gets lost.
5. **Context kept:** when you paste another AI's answer, you say who wrote it (Grok, Boardy, Meta AI).
6. **Closing the loop:** you mark up my last readout inline, so I know what you've seen and what changed.
