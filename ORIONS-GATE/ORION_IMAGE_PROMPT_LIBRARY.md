*Written 6 October 2026 from Beth's Round 13 document. Beth's own prompts are in `ORION_IMAGE_PROMPTS_BETH.md`, and Meta AI's replies are in `ORION_IMAGE_READOUTS_META_AI.md`. This file is the third piece: **ready-to-paste prompts for every storyline, both locked art styles and every platform**.*

**How to use it**
1. Pick a **style block** (§1) and paste it first.
2. Paste the **scene prompt** under it.
3. Swap anything in `[brackets]` for your own characters, art or aesthetic. Every prompt works in any image generator, and none needs Beth's images.
4. Keep the house rules (§0).

The Round 13 layout (Beth's eight titles and the energies) is built into every platform prompt. If a later round changes it, update §0 first.

## 0. House rules for every ORION image

- **ORION** and **WAR** are always in capitals. The titles are The ORION Engine, The Singularity, Elder's Garden, The Playground, The ORION Chronicles, The Ascent, ORION's Gate and UIAR.
- **The five chaos elements** appear somewhere in every image: Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Lightning.
- **The three energies:** KRION is balance (Earth, Water, Air), Kronos is Fire, Orion is Lightning.
  - KRION: Elder's Garden (Earth), The Playground (Water and Air), ORION's Gate.
  - Kronos: The ORION Chronicles (Fire, high kinetic).
  - Orion: The Ascent (Lightning, whimsical knowledge flow).
- **The Singularity:** a black hole with a glowing accretion disc on the outside and **UIAR** on the inside. It's the poly pod you step out of.
- **Write "Grok", not "Groq".** Groq is a different, real company.
- **Child-safe by default.** No gore, no sexual content, no real-world conspiracy imagery. Anything for under-18 spaces stays gentle.
- **Real people:** parody only, clearly labelled as parody, never cruel, and never showing a real person doing something they didn't do as if it were fact.
- **Other people's characters and brands** (MAD, the Super Bowl, Dragon Tales, D&D, Star Wars, Pokémon, *Free Guy*): use them as *inspiration* only, in our own designs. Don't copy logos or characters in anything public. This is a flag, not legal advice.
