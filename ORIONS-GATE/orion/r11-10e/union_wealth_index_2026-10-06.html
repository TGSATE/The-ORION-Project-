PROPOSED MANAGEMENT CHARTER / DRAFT 01

Trust needs rules people can read.

NOT YET ADOPTED

These are proposed operating rules for review, not claims that a fund, governing board or custody arrangement is already in place.

01

A defined purpose

Support eligible service workers and their families. Publish geographic eligibility, family definitions, permitted uses and assessment criteria before enrollment.

02

A named home for the money

Identify the legal operator and financial institution. Keep fund money separate from personal and operating accounts, with reconciled records.

03

Checks on decisions

Propose independent oversight, two-person payment approval, conflict declarations and recusal from awards involving relatives or associates.

04

Award by published criteria

Set family-support priorities, documentation requirements, review steps and an appeal route. Donations do not buy preference.

05

Visible costs and results

Publish fees, administrative spending, aggregate receipts and distributions. Report on funds held without exposing families’ private records.

06

A written reserve policy

Agree liquidity needs, allowable assets, loss limits and review responsibility. No leverage or formula-based allocation is authorized by the supplied charts.

Operator, trustees, provider, supported countries and final award rules: all pending. No tax-deductibility or deposit-insurance claim is made.

How the numbers work

We treat the return as an effective annual rate, convert it to a monthly rate, and apply contributions at each month’s end. Daily contributions are converted using 365 ÷ 12; weekly contributions use 52 ÷ 12. They are not actual daily or weekly transactions. Annual fees are modeled as a percentage reduction in assets: monthly factor = [(1 + annual return) × (1 − annual fee)]^(1/12). Values are rounded only for display. Today's dollars divide the projected balance by (1 + inflation)^years.
