My last review looked at the Master GDD mostly as a game, so I've now re-read it with all three platforms in mind. The most urgent problem: the Accountability Snap needs the suit, and the web and education platforms have no suit. As written, no Master Accountability Record could ever be finalized on two of your three platforms. A signature that needs no special hardware and is tied to the record's content fixes this.
The rules the platforms need where they connect are checked in RequestProject/Submissions/CrossPlatformCheck.lean. It builds with no sorry, no warnings and only Lean's standard axioms, and the whole library still builds. Each model is small and deliberately simplified.
What the proofs show
1. One shared record of work prevents double credit. If each piece of work has one global ID, posting it again on another platform earns nothing (cross_post_earns_nothing, shared_ledger_no_double_count). With a separate record per platform, one course project that is also a Gauntlet deliverable and a community claim would earn credit three times (separate_ledgers_double_count).
1. Web support can be kept out of game progress. If community input only changes cosmetics, a player's progress depends on their game play alone (web_never_moves_progress, same_play_same_progress). A "civic upgrade" with a gameplay effect, such as a faster Cantina or Rind thinning that lowers the Kronos meter, would break this.
1. Point conversions between platforms need a limit. Conversions rounded down can't create value around a loop whose rates multiply to 1 or less (conversion_loop_no_gain). If reputation → flux at 1:1 and flux-backed game publications earn reputation at 2:1, holdings double every lap (conversion_loop_amplifies).
1. The Time-Gate must count every platform if study elsewhere should count. Meeting the learning-to-play ratio on each platform implies meeting it overall (local_gates_imply_global), but not the reverse. A child who studies 10 hours on the education platform and plays 1 hour is still blocked by a game-only gate (global_gate_not_local).
1. One child-safety policy for all platforms. With one shared account record and policy, an adult who isn't a verified educator can't contact a minor anywhere (unverified_adult_never_reaches_minor). The GDD's strict age segregation, as written, also blocks every teacher (strict_segregation_blocks_educators). The education platform needs an exception for verified educators with parental approval.
