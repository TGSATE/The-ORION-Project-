This page places measured groupoid phenomena, K-theoretic assembly obstructions, and coarse non-embeddability side by side. A recurring mechanism Expansion prevents large sets from having small boundaries. In operator language, averaging over expanding moves can produce a projection onto nearly uniform or invariant vectors. In suitable settings, the projection has ghost behavior: its matrix coefficients become small far from the diagonal, even though the operator remains non-compact and can carry a nonzero K-class. Expansion / spectral gap → averaging projection or ghost → a detectable operator-theoretic phenomenon. The final arrow is context-dependent. For dynamical Roe algebras, the projection can characterize expansion in an associated measure. For expander box spaces, a Kazhdan idempotent can obstruct surjectivity of an ℓ p assembly map. For Gromov’s monster construction, embedded expanders obstruct coarse embedding into Hilbert space. Explore groupoids → Assembly and K-theory Here the issue is whether a global Roe-algebra K-class is assembled from localization data. The key contradiction pairs ghost vanishing with trace detection.
