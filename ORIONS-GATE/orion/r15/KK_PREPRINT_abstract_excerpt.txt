                    A torsion-free counterexample to the
                  Kadison–Kaplansky projection conjecture
                                               OpenAI

                                        September 23, 2026


                                               Abstract
       We construct a finitely generated torsion-free discrete group whose reduced group C ∗ -algebra
    contains a projection other than zero and the identity. This disproves the Kadison–Kaplansky
    projection conjecture.
