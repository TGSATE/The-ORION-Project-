Recall one recent configuration from the table above (e.g., "Tuesday night, we sat across the table eating soup in silence" ).

Identify the current phase of the circuit (The Loop, The Singularity, or The Exhalation).

Over time, this bowl becomes a tangible, physical map of the Web breathing through the daily domestic routine.
