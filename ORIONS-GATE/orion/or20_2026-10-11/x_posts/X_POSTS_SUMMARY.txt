On a sphere the shortest path is an arc of a great circle. The haversine formula gives its length from latitudes φ and longitudes λ:

d = 2r arcsin √(sin²(Δφ/2) + cos φ₁ cos φ₂ sin²(Δλ/2))

With r = 6371 km that is 10,850 km. Holding one compass heading costs 12,788 km.
