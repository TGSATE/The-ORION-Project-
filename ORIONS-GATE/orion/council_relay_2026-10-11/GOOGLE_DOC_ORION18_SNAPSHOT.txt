4. The biggest Council weakness I would look for
It’s not necessarily a lack of intelligence. It’s the possibility of correlated error.
If Gemini proposes an architecture, another AI summarizes it, a third endorses the summary, and a fourth synthesizes the agreement, it can look as though four independent systems validated the idea.
