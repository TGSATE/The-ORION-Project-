SOCIAL COMMUNITY PLATFORM 


Based on the provided architecture documents, here is the exhaustive summary of the current state of the Global Social Media Platform.
The social media platform integrates the game's underlying physics engine, post-quantum cryptography, and strict anti-censorship rules to create a mathematically verified, bot-proof public square.
I. Content Visibility & Toxicity Tracking
* Algorithmic Friction Tracking: The platform integrates the game's Asymmetric State Engine to track community friction in real time.
* High Impedance for Rage (Kronos): High-rage, chaotic threads (classified as Pure Kronos) are naturally subjected to high algorithmic impedance, reducing their spread.
* Visibility Boosts for Coherence (Gold Lock): Constructive, balanced discussions that achieve Gold Lock receive a 4\times visibility boost across the global feed.
II. Anti-Censorship & "Soft Hiding" Moderation
* No Hard Deletions: The platform enforces a strict "No Silencing" rule; comments cannot be hard-deleted from the server.
* Permanent Soft Hiding Mechanic: Users and project teams can curate their feeds by soft-hiding comments. This ability never expires and spans all 13 levels of the platform.
* Public Accountability: Hiding a comment requires the user to provide a public reason for the action.
* Raw Transparency: Hiding is strictly a visual filter. Other users can always toggle a disclaimer to bypass the filter and view the raw, archived notes and comments.
* Algorithmic Inclusion: Even when a comment is hidden by a user, the AI Council nodes still take ALL comments (both hidden and visible) into consideration during project audits and evaluations.
III. Identity Verification & Anti-Bot Architecture
* Provenance Badges: The platform eliminates bot swarms and fake experts by displaying cryptographically verified 64-node domain badges directly on user profiles. These badges prove the user's manual human mastery over specific subjects.
* Anti-Spam & Amplification Protection: Every single social interaction (like, comment, tip) is bound to a unique UETR (Unique End-to-end Transaction Reference) and a single-use commit hash.
* Duplicate Error (E_DUP): Attempting to replay an executed transaction or interaction immediately triggers an E_DUP error, permanently neutralizing bot amplification attacks and spam loops.
IV. Cryptographic Post Stamping & Authorship
* Quantum-Resistant Social Identity: Public posts, research papers, and social comments are wrapped in canonical ASCII envelopes.
* Immutable Timestamps: These envelopes are stamped with a post-quantum SHA3-256 hash (qbind), establishing absolute, immutable proof of authorship and creation timestamps that cannot be retroactively spoofed or altered.
* 2026 ETSI Security Compliance: The platform aligns with ETSI TS 119 312 V2.1.1 standards for post-quantum digital signatures, ensuring high-profile user accounts remain completely secure against future quantum decryption attacks.
V. Anonymous Financial Integration
* In-Feed Micro-Tipping & Royalties: Users can financially back independent researchers, tip content creators, or purchase custom blueprints directly within the public social feed.
* Credit Without Identity: These transactions utilize the SOLN1-Q/1 envelope using DGH1 addresses. This allows settlements to process anonymously without exposing the user's civil identity, keeping their financial support completely private from ad-profiling, targeted marketing, or external surveillance.
