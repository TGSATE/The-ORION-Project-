Team workflow · draft procedures · training notes
A clear path from local work to reviewed sharing.
Use these draft procedures to onboard teammates to the profile workspace, private archives, sharing views, and backup workflow. Adapt each SOP to the team’s actual practice before relying on it.
Training and records boundary
Checklist progress, role names, and notes are saved only in this browser. They are editable planning records—not verified training, identity checks, approvals, signatures, or GMP compliance evidence. Do not enter credentials, sensitive personal data, or confidential content.
Recommended team workflow
Prepare → work locally → review → share deliberately → back up
1 · Prepare:  agree who owns the record, what information is public-safe, and where the authoritative source lives.
2 · Work locally:  create profiles, tasks, schedules, and archive notes in the browser. Treat imported content as unverified.
3 · Review:  check file names, image choices, task claims, and recipient scope before creating any share link.
4 · Share deliberately:  send only the selected view or files; tell recipients links contain readable data and do not sync future edits.
5 · Back up:  export a full browser backup before significant changes and keep it somewhere appropriate for its contents.
