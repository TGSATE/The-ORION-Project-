PART I: Human-AI Safety & System Governance Rules

1. The Public Scrutiny & Information Gathering Rule The platform does not prohibit "black box" claims or unsubstantiated theories; limiting information gathering defeats the purpose of the engine. Users are welcome to bring fully heuristic, theoretical, or "spaghetti" hypotheses to the global community.
* Dynamic Labeling: These claims must simply be explicitly identified and tagged under the four-tier epistemic system (Levels A-D) as heuristic/theoretical. It is the specific job of the AI Council to double-check everything against the global knowledge vault, identifying what is verifiable and what contradicts, and ensuring the layers are properly established if the human collaborator fails to do so. These labels are dynamic and will change as a claim's validity is proven or disproven by the community.
* Cascading Transparency & The 3 Publication Gates: Transparency is enforced through a progressive cascade utilizing three hard publication layers to bridge the four Trinity Sets (Gate 1 at Level 4, Gate 2 at Level 7, Gate 3 at Level 10). During active development within a Trinity Set, the team maintains absolute control over their workspace; nothing (chat logs, CAD files, codebases, datasets, or physical assets) becomes public without the team's permission until they officially trigger the next publication gate. At these gates, all project assets from the previous tier become permanently accessible for public viewing and interaction. Note: The AI Council nodes review all private logs and assets during the Gauntlets before they are officially published to the public in the next tier.
2. The External Verification Rule (Human & AI) Any claim presented as an established empirical fact (Level A) must be accompanied by a direct, reputable external resource link verifying the claim. This rule applies equally to humans and AI agents. When an AI comes back verifying information, it must provide the exact external proof, ensuring the framework is grounded in verifiable reality rather than AI hallucination.
3. The Kronos Preservation & Redirection Filter (Zero Information Loss) If a human-AI interaction devolves into a highly intense or non-productive loop, the session ends in a Kronos Termination.
* Preservation: The raw, uncompressed spaghetti phase is never quarantined, hidden, or deleted. It is preserved in its entirety, clearly tagged ([Kronos Termination: Session halted due to XYZ]), and saved so the genuine spark of value buried within the intensity can be extracted when the user is ready.
* Redirection: Upon a Kronos Termination, the user is immediately redirected to global tools available to help with self-regulation and de-escalation. They are presented with opportunities to join classrooms or other projects on shared topics, or given opportunities to explore personal creativity to productively channel their energy.
4. Cognitive Atrophy & The Kronos/Orion States The abdication of human judgment is managed structurally through the natural friction of the system. The system actively combats cognitive atrophy by forcing the user to navigate the oscillating states of Kronos (deconstruction/raw input) and Orion (reconstruction/reframing). The effort required to synthesize raw spaghetti into an Orion Seed ensures the human maintains the cognitive capacity to steer, explicitly preventing the user from relying on the AI to do the thinking for them.
