How to use these diagrams
Use-case view:  actors are outside the system boundary; ovals name goals or operations in the workflow.
Sequence view:  read from top to bottom. Arrows show the order of illustrative messages between participants.
Editable playbook:  adapt the checklist to your team. A saved or shared checklist is guidance, not an authenticated approval or completed action.
