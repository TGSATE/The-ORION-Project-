Privacy Policy
This privacy policy is effective as of March 31, 2026. By using farqueen.com pages, you expressly consent to the practices and activities described in this document. When you use our website, we may collect and store information. We or third party vendors, including Google, may set and use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits. Google's use of the DoubleClick cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites, and/or other websites. Users may opt out of the use of the DoubleClick cookie for interest-based advertising by visiting the  ads preference manager  in Google. You can set your web browser to refuse cookies, but this may limit website functionality. You may also 'opt-out' of behavioral advertising by visiting the  Network Advertising Initiative  opt-out tool. Further, please review the disclosure on  how Google uses data when you use partners’ sites or apps
Log file information is automatically reported by your web browser. Server logs may include your IP address, browser type and language, number of clicks, domain names of websites visited, pages viewed, and the order of those pages, the amount of time spent on particular pages, the date and time. We or third parties may use clear gifs or web beacons, pieces of data used to track the online usage patterns of our users anonymously. In addition, we may also use clear gifs in HTML-based emails sent to our users to track which emails are opened. Information collected through clear gifs, cookies, and log file information is largely non-personally identifiable information.
Third-Party Providers
Third-party service-based information may be collected. This includes linked services that you authorize to share information. Other third-party supported services, such as those authorized by Google, may collect information through various methods. Such information is subject to the privacy policy of each such third-party service. The farqueen.com privacy policy does not apply to third-party advertisers. We may share your information or data with third parties. We may store personal information in servers or databases co-located with hosting providers. We or third parties may remember your information to provide custom, personalized content and information, track effectiveness of marketing, access your information after sign in, market our service to advertisers and other third parties, and improve design via new features by tracking your preferences and trends.
We may allow third-party ad servers or ad networks to serve advertisements and collect information. These third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology to send, directly to your browser, the advertisements and links, automatically receiving your IP address when this happens. They may also use other technologies to measure the effectiveness of their advertisements and to personalize advertising content. farqueen.com does not provide any personally identifiable information to these third-party ad servers or ad networks without your consent. In addition, third-party advertisers may use information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide ads.
Release of Information
Any information or user content submitted uploaded becomes publicly available and can be collected and used by other users, who may forward this information or user content to other websites or people. Be aware that if your user content includes personal information, such as your name and email address, or other data, the public will have access to it. Also, all photographs uploaded reside on public URLs, and may be used by any person who knows the URL location. Please do not upload any photos or personal data that you do not want the public to share freely, and have access to.
We may release collected information if required to do so by law, to comply with state and federal laws or by court order, or other law enforcement request. We may disclose collected information to the Internal Revenue Service and third party payment providers, for tax-related purposes. We may also share collected information in order to investigate illegal activities, including but not limited to, fraud; potential threats to public safety or the physical safety, property, or rights of any person or group. We strive to protect your information and other data. However, we cannot guarantee the security of any information that is being transmitted over the internet. As a result, you provide all data at your own risk. Please  contact us  with any questions or comments.
