A. A human right
B. Instinctual
C. An allusion
D. An illusion

A. San Francisco
B. Santa Cruz
C. Big Sur
D. Hollywood
3. Who wrote 'Man and His Symbols'?
A. Milton Erickson
B. Carl Jung
C. Fritz Perls
D. Virginia Satir

A. Pavlov
B. Skinner
C. Freud
D. Piaget
