1. Foundational Governance & Safety Principles
Human-AI interaction across the ORION Engine platform is governed by a non-negotiable principle: Humans hold the stakes; AI carries the scale. AI agents operate strictly as non-sovereign execution proxies, cognitive mirrors, and computational accelerators. They are permanently barred from exercising moral judgment, executive authorization, or creative ownership.
Steer-by-Wire Operational Model
Human oversight transitions from continuous physical friction to an automated steer-by-wire paradigm. While AI agents are delegated path-plotting, data-sifting, and multi-agent pattern matching, they must halt at mandatory re-authorization gates before taking any consequential action. Humans maintain absolute kill-switch authority at every level of execution.
Undilutable Equal-Stake Liability
AI agents carry zero legal or systemic liability. In any party, research swarm, corporate guild, or collaborative team, liability is borne exclusively and equally across all human participants. For a team of n human members, each human holds an un-dilutable liability share of exactly:

Anti-Atrophy & Sharpness Accounting
To prevent human cognitive atrophy—the abdication of human critical thinking to automated systems—the platform actively measures whether a human exits an interaction more discerning than when they entered. If a user attempts to delegate core thinking, moral reasoning, artistic creation, or educational problem-solving to an AI, the system triggers a hard operational refusal.
Zero Information Loss & Kronos Preservation
When human-AI interactions enter high-friction, repetitive, or emotionally intense loops, the system executes a Kronos Termination. The raw, uncompressed interaction ("spaghetti phase") is never quarantined, hidden, or deleted. It is preserved in its entirety, tagged with provenance metadata ([Kronos Termination: Session halted due to XYZ]), and stored so the user can extract underlying insights when emotionally regulated. The user is immediately redirected to self-regulation tools, educational classrooms, or shared creative projects.

Every major claim, schematic deployment, or experimental execution approval must be filed as an un-deletable Master Accountability Record detailing: the proposer, supporting evidence, strongest objection, uncertainties, and the designated human approver.
To authorize a high-stakes record, the designated human approver must execute an Accountability Snap: holding their breath (verified via biometric sensors) while inputting a dynamic, one-time UI sequence on their Universal Stone/HUD. This physical 2FA ensures approvals are conscious and deliberate, completely blocking automated or accidental sign-offs.
PII Scrubbing & The Sanitized Proposal Shield
* Automated PII Scrubber: An automated privacy filter scans all project logs prior to public publication, permanently redacting real-world personally identifiable information (PII) such as phone numbers, physical addresses, or legal names.
* Sanitized Proposal Shield: Teams can extract project schematics into a formal Proposal Document for Council review, keeping raw brainstorming banter and internal team chat logs sealed in their private team vault. The proposal is tagged in the public registry as [Missing Kronos/Orion Conversation Log] and evaluated strictly on schematic merit.
Dispute Resolution: Discovery Protocol & Administrative Holds
When a live project causes severe friction, coding exploits, or ecosystem harm:
1. Administrative Hold: Galactic Devs/Admins place a hold on the project, temporarily hiding public access on the server.
2. Absolute Log Protection: Devs/Admins cannot forcibly seize or read private chat logs.
3. Voluntary Discovery: Project managers selectively disclose technical evidence, specific log excerpts, or expert testimony to defend their build. The Council renders its verdict based strictly on the evidence voluntarily provided.
4. Resolution or Rework: If evidence is insufficient, the build remains on hold, and the team receives a rework directive to fix the broken logic before public access can be restored.
