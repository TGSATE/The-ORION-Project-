CHANGES/ADDITIONS SUMMARY
Governance, Education, & Mentorship
The educational structure strictly separates academic progression from social interaction. Academic advancement operates purely on merit, allowing a brilliant eight-year-old to tackle high-school-level spatial puzzles in the Quantum Classroom, while social instances remain locked behind age restrictions and a hard PG-13 firewall to protect younger demographics from mature sandbox mechanics. The curriculum employs Montessori-inspired tactile play, where players learn by transmuting soft bubbles into digital Legos and magnetic blocks. For early guidance, mentorship replaces real-world religious figures with lore-specific "System Architects," and veteran players are empowered to publish their highly trained AI Copilots to act as bespoke, public Game Masters. Furthermore, all player-made factions—from corporate cities to spiritual guilds—operate under universal community building guidelines and NewKin Council voting loops, which include the bureaucratic ability to submit active demolition permits to clear abandoned properties.
AI Labor & The Knowledge Economy
* Single-Task Delegation: Players can earn infinite AI Copilots, but each is strictly restricted to a single task; an AI assigned as a Game Master or tutor is entirely locked out of standard gameplay until the job is toggled off. To manage large swarms, players assign an AI Project Manager to act as a board member making delegation decisions.
* Council Tutoring: AFK tutoring naturally operates at half speed, but players can assign a full council of up to 10 AI tutors—each contributing 10% efficiency—to achieve maximum automated training speed.
* Active Mastery Testing: Veteran players can bypass AFK time-gates for their Alt characters by actively participating in generative tutoring sessions. By dynamically answering multiple-choice or short-response questions, they ingest the curriculum in massive chunks without relying on static cheat sheets.
* Curated Cartography: The HUD operates under a complete fog of war. Map knowledge requires dedicated tutoring sessions to exchange, or players can download specific GPS-style routes at central hubs that only reveal the path and a slight topographical buffer for landmarks.
Wilderness Survival & Travel Mechanics
* Teleportation Lockouts: Permanent teleportation keys are exclusively unlocked via physical foot travel from a known homebase. If a player utilizes a temporary teleportation token sent by a friend, they are explicitly locked out of acquiring the permanent key for that destination on that trip. Alts accessing a node via temporary passes can retrieve their permanent key upon arrival, and massive guild runs can check in at stations mid-journey to maintain their teleportation loop.
* Zone Mastery: Collecting all dev-created core teleportation keys within a specific dodecahedron skybox grants instant, anywhere-to-anywhere fast travel for that region.
* Inflatable Outposts: Explorers manage their physical 64-node stats and Kronos/Orion alignment by crafting or purchasing single-use temporary encampments. These range from simple campfires to elite pavilions, are permanently capped at standard Outpost size, and are strictly limited by the collision parameters of the immediate topography.
* Nested Infinite Scaling: The universe expands inward via fractal dodecahedron skyboxes. Players can craft portal objects to generate custom skyboxes where they act as local Devs, maintaining civic control over their core solar system while permanently forfeiting governance of any subsequent sub-skyboxes generated within their outpost worlds.
Bio-Engineering & Planetary Ecosystems
* Procedural Darwinism: The physical topology and climate shaped by the Player-Dev actively mold the evolutionary baseline of native species. Creatures evolve through 64 distinct levels mirroring the 64-node anatomical grid, with sub-64-node creatures running on hidden AI directives guiding them toward sentience.
* Course Correction & Sentience Lockdown: Devs can passively watch evolution or actively pause at each level to bio-engineer and course-correct misaligned nodes. Once the first species achieves sentience, planetary evolution locks down, and the engine generates a single Easter egg key for other species to randomly discover.
* Neanderthal Integration: Secondary species finding the Easter egg awaken as proto-civilizations (Neanderthals); the Dev must physically study them to lock in their 64-node grid and generatively integrate them as a playable race. Divergent evolution applies if a pre-sentient creature is taken to a different skybox to evolve.
* The Tether & Progenitor XP: A skybox's harvesting and crafting buffs are dictated by the collective Kronos/Orion alignment of its native species. The Dev can utilize a "Tether Toggle" to sever the galactic connection and protect against external griefing, though local actions continually affect the immediate environment. To reward creation, whenever a new player adopts a public custom race (unlocked via travel or corporate advertisements), the Progenitor Dev receives passive XP distributed directly into their biological, crafting, and anatomical stats.
