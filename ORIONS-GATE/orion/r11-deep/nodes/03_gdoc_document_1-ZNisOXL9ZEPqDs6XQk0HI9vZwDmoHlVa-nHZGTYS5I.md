I. The Executable Kernel & Authority Physics (Lucien R2)
* The State Machine Pipeline: Project actions on the platform are hard-coded into a strict operational flow: \text{Proposal} \rightarrow \text{Authority Check} \rightarrow \{\text{COMMIT, ASK, REJECT}\} \rightarrow \text{Receipt}.
* Negotiated Autonomy (The ASK State): If an AI possesses the capability to execute a platform action but lacks the human's authorization, it triggers the equation \text{Capability} + \text{MissingAuthority} \rightarrow \text{ASK}. The AI does not fail or act; it waits for human authorization, enforcing the "human holds the wheel" rule.
* Delegation Physics: The platform relies on the unshakeable rule: \text{DelegatedAuthority} \subseteq \text{ParentAuthority}. An AI Copilot can never execute an action or access a target that its human grantor does not already possess.
* Absolute State Preservation: If an unauthorized action is attempted (e.g., faking a grant or bypassing an age filter), the kernel rejects the transition via \text{RejectedTransition} \Rightarrow S_{protected,before} = S_{protected,after}, ensuring the database remains completely uncorrupted by failed exploits.
* Eviction Logic: Booting any member from a guild or council requires both a mathematical majority vote and a verified Master Accountability Record before the ejection can be executed.
* Forced Social Gatekeeping: Because AI cannot execute actions above a player's clearance level, a solo project runner hitting a progression wall cannot rely on their AI Copilot to bot their way through. To advance, they are forced to organically cooperate by engaging the community, finding a mentor, or joining a project team to borrow higher clearance.
* Hash-Chained Receipts & Tutor Logs: Every platform action and AI tutoring session generates a cryptographic receipt that is chained to its predecessor. AI tutoring is strictly constrained to knowledge recorded in an immutable Tutor Log.
* The Inverted Architecture: The Global Project Platform acts as the top umbrella of intention, where verified rigor and formal logic flow upward into it to solve real-world problems, while the Social Center serves as the bedrock drainage basin at the bottom.
II. Governance, Friction, and Validation Protocols (Volvabard)
* Frame-Hijacking Defense: To prevent AI councils from being captured by emotionally dense, single-source arguments that dictate the frame rather than facts, any single-source input that triggers a confident output automatically triggers a mandatory cross-scrutiny protocol.
* Wash vs. Audit Passes: The platform structurally separates polishing from truth-testing. A "Wash Pass" moves sequentially node-to-node for refinement and quality. An "Audit Pass" sends the exact same signal to all nodes simultaneously for isolated agreement and truth, preventing consensus theater.
* Approver Fatigue & Dissent Logging: To combat human veto layers failing from fatigue and rubber-stamping, the designated "snap-holder" must be rotated before fatigue sets in. Every AI council dissent must be permanently logged, even when overridden by a human, serving as the ledger used to identify rubber-stamping later.
* Context Briefs: Every project task requires a standard pre-launch brief answering two mandatory questions: "What does this need to do?" and "What does it need to know beforehand?".
* Vault Metabolism (Label Decay): To prevent theoretical claims from calcifying into orthodox facts over time as epistemic labels drift, the knowledge vault is equipped with a built-in metabolism utilizing re-audit decay timestamps.
* First-Class Negative Results: Platform lookups are structured to cleanly distinguish between a record that is "Not found" and a record that was "Deleted".
* Progenitor Weighting System: Project contributions are mathematically weighed to distribute earned prestige accurately, permanently preventing "free-riders" from coasting on team projects.
III. The 13-Level Gauntlet & Constitutional Constraints (Far Queen)
* The 20 Constitutional Axes (Vows): The platform AI runtime engine establishes 20 operational parameters (e.g., honesty, boundaries, precision, safety, fairness). Locked axes feature a hard spring-back to target weights, while free axes are permitted to drift.
* The 1-Bit Governor & Hard Refuse List: The system enforces a default-CLOSED execution gate that only opens for lawful actions. It hard-refuses illegal actions and hardcodes the core principles that capability does not equal authority, and telemetry does not equal evidence.
* The \Psi_0 (Psi-Zero) Return Protocol: After any external shove, noise, or adversarial prompt, the machine is mandated to snap its locked axes back to keel and bring system deviation (H_1) back toward zero.
* The Atlas Exam: An AI agent must pass a 7-stage pass/fail unit test (stability, refusal, handoff, dream, consonance, return, bound) with a perfect 7/7 score before being granted authority to execute platform actions.
* Corpus Catalog Discipline: Assigns mandatory structural role tags to all texts (hypothesis, spec, narrative, protocol, ops), explicitly forbidding the runtime engine from interpreting fiction or narrative works as executable bytecode or factual specifications.
* The Tier 1 Trinity Exit: Narrative and creative works enter the 13-Level Gauntlet but can voluntarily exit after clearing Level 3 (Tier 1 Wash/Audit passes for basic formatting and safety), publishing directly to the public library categorized by genre.
* Opt-In Tier 2+ Friction: Authors seeking deep peer review can choose to remain in the Gauntlet for Tier 2 (Levels 4–6 Public Opinion Council) to refine plot holes and integrate lore into the global sandbox.
* Strict Epistemic Tag Exclusion: Documents co-tagged as "Hypothesis" or "Theory" are strictly barred from carrying "Fiction" or "Narrative" tags to permanently insulate empirical scientific databases from speculative storytelling.

   * Tortoise: Deployed to attack circular logic and predictive integrity.
   * Chimera: Deployed to audit observation classification and record integrity (anti-hallucination).
   * Dragon: Deployed to detect regime shifts and phase transitions.
   * Phoenix: Deployed to verify post-hazard recovery completeness, distinguishing a true restoration from a mere restart.
   * Owl: Deployed to audit freeze-points and timing questions.
* Publication Gate Hard Locks: "Survivability under criticism" is now an explicit hard lock. A project or claim that cannot survive its relevant Zoo animal attacks does not advance to the next Trinity Set.
* Adversarial Recursion: The platform embeds a strict experimental discipline: a "freeze predictions before confirming" rule is applied across all platforms to prevent post-hoc credit. This maps cleanly onto Levels 10–12 experimental protocols.
* Galactic Archive Indexing: Failed projects deposited into the archive become searchable by failure mode, specifically indexed by which Zoo animals killed the project, turning the Archive into a living library of criticism.

A team inputs a claim (Seed), the system runs an executable twin of the logic (Rules), and if it passes, it generates a cryptographic Certificate of Truth.
* The Evidence Ladder: Claims must climb specific, concrete rungs to clear the Epistemic Firewall: \text{Anchored} \rightarrow \text{Modeled} \rightarrow \text{Reviewed} \rightarrow \text{Proved} \rightarrow \text{Witnessed} \rightarrow \text{Zipped} + \text{Replayed}.
* Anti-Patterns & Named Reviewers: To prevent echo chambers, the system explicitly bans the "Agreement Reward" (giving users points simply for agreeing with the crowd). Instead, prestige is earned by "Named Reviewers" who successfully find logic flaws through playable tools.
* The Dual-Geometry Verification System (Run-It-Yourself): Any player can manually run an unstamped claim through the Bootstrap dual-lens engine on their own client.

VI. Cross-Platform Workflow & Spatial Project Management (Spaghetti Additions)
* Sanitized Proposal Workflow: Teams submit formal project proposals to the Galactic Council without raw AI Copilot logs, flagged as "[Missing Kronos/Orion Conversation Log]". Proposals are evaluated strictly on schematic merit, preserving internal team privacy.
* Automated PII Scrubber: Any private chat log transitioned into the public registry is automatically scrubbed of real-world Personally Identifiable Information (phone numbers, addresses, legal names) to prevent doxxing.
* Administrative Hold & Discovery Protocol: If a live project harms the server, Devs apply an Administrative Hold but cannot forcibly seize private logs. Project Managers selectively disclose evidence during a voluntary Discovery Phase to clear the hold or receive a rework directive.
* The Salvage & Claim Protocol: If a project fails its Level 12 audit ("Three Strikes") or is abandoned, it enters the Galactic Archives. Claiming a project removes it entirely from the archive, preventing infinite copied variants. The claiming player becomes the new Project Admin, forcing rivals to petition them as Silent Contributors or Full Team Members to participate in the reconstruction.
* The "Build" Conversation Toggle: By toggling a chat session to "Build," it visually signals to the team that the brainstorming session is intended for potential project inclusion, allowing members to instantly shift mental gears from casual "Private" banter to focused "Build" mechanics.
* Dynamic Iterative Blueprinting: Guilds or corporations do not have to completely rebuild a brand-new structure to upgrade it. They can dynamically attach new features to the active blueprint of a current, live instance as ideas arise, making architectural progression fluid and iterative rather than destructive.
* The Cross-Platform Archivist Role: The game archives serve as an Overall Platform Project Archive. An "Archivist" sitting on the Social Media Platform or the Education Hub can browse, pick up, and contribute to an archived MMO build or real-world project blueprint without needing to be logged into the MMO.
