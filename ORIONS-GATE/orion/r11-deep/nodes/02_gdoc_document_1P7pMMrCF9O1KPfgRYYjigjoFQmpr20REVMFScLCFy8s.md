ADDITIONAL QUESTIONS, COMMENTS, CONCERNS


An audit of the entire master architecture reveals several explicit unresolved technical questions, open theoretical gaps, and critical operational roadblocks across the collaborator contributions that must be resolved to finalize the system.
I. Unresolved Questions & Systemic Roadblocks
1. Methodological & Game Design Gaps (Matthew / Monolithic Zoo)
* The Complete 64-Node Zoo Mapping: While the primary 4 animals have been assigned to quadrants (Iron: Tortoise, Gold: Phoenix, Copper: Chimera, Silver: Owl), the remaining 8+ primary Zoo operators (Ant, Bat, Beaver, Bison, Bonobo, Butterfly, Cat, Chameleon, Cobra, Termite, Axolotl) need formal mapping into the 64-node anatomical grid so players can equip specific critical temperaments into their 5 nested mod slots.
* 3D Proof Arena Visualization: The exact visual and mechanical representation of Zoo attack vectors during a "Boss Fight" verification sequence remains undefined. We must specify how an attack by the Phoenix (testing post-hazard recovery vs. mere restart) or the Tortoise (testing circular logic) visually manifests in a 3D holographic environment.
* Algorithmic Swarm Behaviors: The exact programmatic output differences between evolving AI swarms infused with different Zoo behavioral DNA need definition. For example, how does a Termite/Ant swarm (focused on collective construction) programmatically differ in its skybox building algorithms from an Owl swarm (focused on freeze-points and timing)?
2. Cryptographic & Formal Verification Gaps (Mike DuPont / Bootstrap)
* The Undefined Credit Scheme: Mike’s Pattern Language framework leaves the credit and value-routing scheme open. When Team A generates a Rung 4 (Proved) Certificate and Team B utilizes that certificate to construct a real-world physical engine or live MMO skybox, how does the system automatically route Progenitor XP, royalties, or Toroidal Flux back to Team A's SHA-256 Anchor?
* Lean Soundness Extraction Gap: There is an unproved gap between the executable twin (the compiled C/WebAssembly checker) and the formal Lean theorem. The bridge proving that the compiled bytecode strictly guarantees the Lean mathematical proof must be mathematically sealed.
* Browser Lean Footprint: Running a full Lean formal checker client-side inside standard web browsers or mobile devices remains computationally untested.
* Anchor Hash Storage Without Blockchain Bloat: We must formalize how SHA-256 Anchor hashes and receipt chains are stored on platform servers across interplanetary distances (\Delta t) without creating the data bloat of a traditional blockchain or breaking lightweight client synchronization.
3. Execution Kernel & Runtime Vulnerabilities (Lucien)
* v0.3 Adversarial Stress-Testing: The current kernel is a local v0.2 proof-of-concept. The architecture still needs live multiplayer server implementation, persistent database integration, and adversarial stress-testing against vote-forging, grant-replay attacks, and Tutor Log tampering.
4. Operational, Legal, and Infrastructure Roadblocks
* The Fiat/KYC vs. "Identity Hole" Paradox: The SOLN1-Q/1 protocol processes micro-grants and funding confidentially using identity_of=UNSIGNED (the "Identity Hole") on real-world banking rails (ISO 20022 / SEPA). We must define how the platform satisfies real-world global KYC (Know Your Customer) and AML (Anti-Money Laundering) compliance without compromising the user's frontend shield or storing PII.
* Parental Verification Without PII Hoarding: The Under-18 Bubble Room and Time-Gate Engine require verified parental consent. We must establish a method to legally prove guardianship without holding high-risk, real-world identity files on central servers.
* Server Hosting & Cost Allocation: Nested 120-node dodecahedron skyboxes permit infinite overlapping spaces. If a Player-Dev generates a massive, persistent universe inside a Genesis Portal, who pays for the server compute and storage?
* Hardware Rollout Strategy: The system references a physical 64-node conductive biometric suit alongside a software fallback. We must decide whether to launch software-first (mouse/keyboard/biometric fallbacks) or tie launch to dedicated hardware manufacturing.

* Result: The banking rail verifies that the sender is a real, sanctioned-cleared human without ever seeing or transmitting the user's legal identity, preserving total compliance alongside absolute privacy.
