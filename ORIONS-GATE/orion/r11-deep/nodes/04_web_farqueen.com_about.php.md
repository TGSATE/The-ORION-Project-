About Us
farqueen.com is an educational platform, incorporating adaptive ai tutoring. Beyond tutoring academic subjects, the system will offer psychological testing, career placement evaluation, skill certification, unbiased AI performance evaluations, comprehensive college entrance preparation, and job applicant screening.
Part of a broader system inviting professors, teachers, tutors, students, companies, governments, and the public worldwide to share lesson plans and content. Contributors may gain recognition, backlinks, and potential payment, funded by sponsorships, affiliate links, partnerships, logo placement, product tie-ins, foundational grants, corporations, and government support. The platform envisions a global network of physical learning centers, either replacing traditional schools or integrating into existing curricula, depending on local government objectives.
Choosing a Major
You may pick your major based on values that you hold. Choosing a major based on your core beliefs can lead to work that is more rewarding and encouraging. However, attending university is a time of experimentation and self-discovery, and most people solidify their values during their college years. At first glance, your passions seem just like interest areas, only stronger. But this is quite an understatement. Passions are areas of deep interest, sure, but they also incorporate your values and abilities into something that becomes a burning, lifelong desire.
