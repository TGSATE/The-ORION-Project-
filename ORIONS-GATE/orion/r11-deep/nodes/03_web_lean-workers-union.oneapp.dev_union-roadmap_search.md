Data and trust boundaries
Local changes are saved in this browser's local storage. A cookie is not used for this research store; URL links and JSON files are the portable/sneakernet options. Links may become long and reveal their contents to anyone who receives them. Do not include secrets or private personal data. Imported findings are not authenticated or independently verified.
