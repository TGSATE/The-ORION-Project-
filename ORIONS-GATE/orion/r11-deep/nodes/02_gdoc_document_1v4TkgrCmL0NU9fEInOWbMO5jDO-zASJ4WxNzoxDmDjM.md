SECTION 1: SYSTEM GOVERNANCE & HUMAN-AI SAFETY (PART I)
The micro-level interaction and safety framework guarantees human cognitive sovereignty, eliminates AI hallucinations, enforces strict liability, and prevents human skill atrophy.
1. 1-to-1 Sovereign Identity Architecture
* Single Sovereign Identity: Unlike the multi-persona 20-character limit used in the MMO sandbox, the Global Project Platform operates strictly on a 1-to-1 Sovereign Human Identity.
* Aesthetic Avatar Skins: Users may assign different visual avatar skins across platforms, but all underlying credentials, portfolio merits, titles, and legal liabilities remain permanently bound to their single verified real-world human identity.
2. The 4-Tier Epistemic Labeling System & Public Scrutiny Rule
* Unsubstantiated Input Allowed: The platform never prohibits "black box" claims, speculative theories, or raw heuristic "spaghetti" phase dumps. Limiting raw information gathering defeats the engine's purpose.
* Dynamic Epistemic Labeling (Levels A–D): The AI Council automatically tags all incoming claims dynamically against the global knowledge vault:
   * Level A (Empirical Fact): Verified empirical truths backed by direct external citations.
   * Levels B–D (Heuristic / Theoretical / Unverified): Speculative claims, unverified models, or raw intuitive dumps. Labels adjust dynamically as the community proves or disproves claims.
3. The External Verification Rule
* Mandatory Citation Mapping: Any claim presented as an established empirical fact (Level A) must be accompanied by a direct, reputable external resource link verifying the claim.
* Parity Between Human & AI: Applies equally to human users and AI agents. When an AI agent asserts verified data, it must supply the exact external proof to prevent AI hallucinations.
4. Kronos Preservation & Redirection Filter (Zero Information Loss)
* Kronos Termination Trigger: If a human-AI interaction or group session devolves into a highly intense, looping, or non-productive state, the system executes a Kronos Termination.
* Absolute Preservation: Raw, uncompressed brainstorming ("spaghetti") is never deleted, hidden, or quarantined. It is saved in its entirety, explicitly tagged ([Kronos Termination: Session halted due to XYZ]), and preserved so the core spark of value can be extracted later.
* Constructive Redirection: Upon termination, the user is redirected to self-regulation tools, relevant educational classrooms, or shared collaborative projects to productively channel their energy.
5. Cognitive Atrophy Prevention & State Navigation
* Friction-Driven Engagement: Combats human cognitive atrophy by forcing users to actively navigate the oscillating energy states of Kronos (deconstruction/raw input) and Orion (reconstruction/reframing).
* Prohibition of Human Abdication: The effort required to synthesize raw intuition into a structured "Orion Seed" ensures the human maintains the cognitive capacity to steer, explicitly blocking the AI from doing the thinking, decision-making, or creative synthesis for the human.
6. Active AI Refusal & The Dual Emergency Stop ("Big Red Button")
* AI Big Red Stop Button: If an AI agent encounters an anomaly or unintended result outside the explicitly defined scope of a project (regardless of whether the AI perceives it as beneficial or harmful), it must immediately hit its internal Big Red Stop Button, halt execution, and alert human operators.
* Human Emergency Brake: Any user observing a live project, simulation, or experiment can flag a critical flaw or safety hazard, acting as a human emergency stop to pause execution for immediate council review.
7. Steer-by-Wire Delegation & Distributed Liability
* Steer-by-Wire Principle: "Humans hold the stakes; AI carries the scale". Human oversight shifts from continuous manual friction to active monitoring with mandatory re-authorization gates and absolute kill-switch authority.
* Equal Weight Stake Liability (1/(n+1) Share): Liability never falls onto a single delegator. AI agents carry zero liability. Every human member of a project team carries an un-dilutable, equal share of liability. For n human members, each human's share is exactly 1/(n+1) when a new member joins.
8. The Master Accountability Record
* Immutable Decision Logging: Every major claim, schematic change, or execution approval must be filed as a formal, un-deletable written record detailing:
   1. The claim and who proposed it.
   2. The supporting evidence.
   3. The strongest objection.
   4. What changed and what remains uncertain.
   5. The specific designated human team member approving the action.
* Strict Approval Rules: Records lacking evidence or stated objections are rejected. AI can propose claims but can never approve. Revisions cannot overwrite old records; they must be filed as brand-new records. Whoever approves carries equal liability share alongside the team.
9. Privacy, Sanitized Proposals, and the Discovery Protocol
* Automated Real-World PII Scrubber: To prevent accidental doxxing, an automated privacy filter scans all project logs prior to public publication, permanently redacting real-world personally identifiable information (PII) such as phone numbers, legal names, or addresses.
* The Sanitized Proposal Shield: Teams can extract project schematics into a formal Proposal Document for Council review, keeping raw brainstorming banter and internal team chat logs sealed in their private team vault (flagged in the registry as [Missing Kronos/Orion Conversation Log]).
* Administrative Holds & Voluntary Discovery:
   * If a live project causes severe friction or exploits, Devs/Admins issue an Administrative Hold, temporarily hiding public access.
   * Devs/Admins cannot forcibly seize private chat logs. Project managers selectively disclose evidence during a voluntary Discovery Phase to clear the hold or receive a rework directive.
