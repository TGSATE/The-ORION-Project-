### 🌉 1. The Apple ↔ Google bridge: full game plan in `APPLE_GOOGLE_BRIDGE_GAME_PLAN.md`

**Why they won't talk**
- Photo storage is a money-maker for both companies.
- Keeping your memories inside their own system makes it scary to switch phones.
- Photos are the most private data there is, and if a bridge leaks, the company gets the blame.
- **Google closed its own side door on 31 March 2025.** Outside apps can no longer read your whole Google Photos library, only photos you pick by hand. That's why Grok said it can't get in.
- **Apple has never had a public door** into iCloud Photos at all.

**They DO cooperate when the reason is good enough**
- 2020: the joint COVID-19 Exposure Notification system.
- The Data Transfer Initiative, where Apple and Google both sit.
- 2023: a shared anti-stalking tracker standard.
- 12 January 2026: Google's Gemini models now help power Apple's Siri.
- Apple's copy of iCloud Photos into Google Photos already exists. **It's just one-time only**: new photos don't follow.

**The pitch, "The Family Photo Bridge," is three small asks**
1. **Keep it in sync.** Let the copy that already exists repeat every week, in both directions.
2. **A read-only key for my own helper.** Let me approve, and cancel any time, an assistant of my choice (from DTI's vetted list of trusted services) to see my own photos.
3. **Shared albums for mixed-phone families.**

**Who to send it to, in order**

2. Both companies' feedback pages.
3. A petition.
