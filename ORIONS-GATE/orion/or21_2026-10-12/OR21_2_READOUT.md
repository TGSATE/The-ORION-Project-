## You asked → here it is

### 🌉 1. "Find us a bridge": the honest answer, and what works today (full version: `APPLE_GOOGLE_BRIDGE_GAME_PLAN.md`, new section 11)
**Nobody can build a bridge that changes the iPhone.** When an app asks for a photo, it opens Apple's own photo window, which is built into iOS. Claude told you the same thing, and I checked it.

**You don't have to pay Apple, and you don't need the space.** You never move the whole library. You only need the one or two photos you're using right now. Cheapest first:
1. **Gmail already has a Google Photos button.** Write an email, tap the paperclip, then **Google Photos**, and pick from your albums. Nothing gets saved to the phone. If you don't see it, update Gmail.
2. **Copy and paste.** In Google Photos, tap **Share**, then **Copy**. Then long-press the box in the other app and tap **Paste**. Nothing gets saved to the phone. *I couldn't test this on a phone, so try it once.*
3. **Share straight out of Google Photos** to the app, if it shows up in the share row.
4. **Save one, use it, delete it,** then empty "Recently Deleted". That takes a few MB for a minute.
5. **Tell the app's makers:** *"Please add a Google Photos picker, like Gmail has."* That's how Gmail got one.

**🛠️ The bridge your team CAN build.** Google has a free, official **Photos Picker API**, a "Choose from Google Photos" button that any app or web page can add. You pick photos by hand, and only those cross over. The team can put that button into Ghost Writer and every ORION page, so ORION never asks you for Apple Photos. It's written up as quest **Q10** in `CODER_BUILD_BRIEF_GHOSTWRITER.md`, a draft for you to send. DEV image prompt **#29** has a mock-up.

**🧾 Audit of the Auditor:** Claude was right on every point I could check, with one small fix. Whether an app shows up when you share from Google Photos is up to *that app's* makers, not a deal between Apple and Google. The table is in section 11.
