**Ground rules for these sheets:**
- **PRIVATE DRAFT.** Not for publication. Each member edits or deletes their own sheet before anything goes public.
- **Only what members shared themselves** in their own docs, posts or the ORION pages. Nothing is inferred from photos or guessed.
- **No health, money, family or address details.** Those stay off every sheet, even when a member mentioned them. Beth's own are listed separately for her review in `PERSONAL_DETAILS_REVIEW_BETH.md`.
- **Role (Beth, Round 12):**
  - **Admins**, with authority in Beth's group: **Beth, Matthew, Mike, Lucien**.
  - **Contributors only**, not admin: **Far Queen (Bobby), Triss, Marek**.
  - Other names are leads, not members.
- Each sheet has a **Council** block. Beth: "they will have their tokens on their council members' avatar sheets". Every player has 5 AI council members, and managing their tokens is part of learning.
